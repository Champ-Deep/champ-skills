# Temporal reference study

## Evidence boundary

Public reference files were decoded and inspected as timestamped 1fps sequences. This establishes scene content and changes, not a full audiovisual watch-through or precise easing/cut timing. The video-analysis endpoint failed. The Captions demo assets are silent; do not invent observations about their sound. Public views measure reach, not B2B conversion or the causal value of an editing technique.

## Performance caveat

At retrieval on 10 November 2026 IST: Hormozi Hooks 91,899 views; Hormozi CLOSER 30,569; Martell prompting 113,657. Martell was highest displayed-view item among 12 recent channel shorts sampled, not an all-time/global ranking. The Hooks video's 4,000-to-850,000 story refers to another edit in its anecdote, not its own observed views. These are business/founder education examples, not a representative enterprise B2B corpus.

## Editorial gates

- Classify the source as explanation, event recap, invitation, demonstration or story before selecting a treatment. An invitation promising lessons does not contain those lessons; ask for teaching footage rather than manufacture the missing content.
- Read the actual speech before storyboarding. Prefer meaning-bearing hook selection and compression over automated decoration.
- Treat low-level silence detection as candidate generation only. Ambient noise and music can hide speech pauses, and zero silence hits does not prove no editorial dead space.
- A successful explainer visual represents relationships, changes or evidence that remain understandable without reading every subtitle. A bigger colored word is emphasis, not an explanation.
- Keep diagrams illustrative unless they represent verified real data or product behavior. Never invent failure assignments, outcomes, screens or metrics to fill B-roll slots.
- Require a short proof edit with actual cuts, explanation, a motivated scene change and audio checks before rolling a new style out to a batch.
