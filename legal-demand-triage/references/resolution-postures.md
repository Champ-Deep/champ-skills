# Resolution postures

Decision table keyed on **what has actually happened**, verified on the docket, not on what the letter threatens. Owners: `counsel` = external counsel in the entity's jurisdiction, `insurer` = cyber/E&O carrier, `site` = whoever owns the property, `Deep` = decision only, never contact with the sender.

| Posture | How to verify | Actions | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Demand only, nothing filed | Court docket search by sender name plus your entity returns nothing | No reply. Send the verdict table to counsel for a one-shot scoped review (flat fee, not open-ended). Calendar a docket re-check in 30 days. Notify the insurer if the policy requires notice of circumstances. Fix hygiene items. | Deep approves, counsel reviews, site fixes |
| Complaint drafted / attached but not filed | Letter says so; docket still empty | Same as above, but treat the draft as a discovery preview: assume the filed version carries every count, so the verdict table must answer all of them. | counsel |
| Filed, not served | Docket shows a case; no proof of service on you | Watch for service. Do not appear early. Prepare the demurrer / motion-to-dismiss outline now (statute's current text, amendment effective and retroactivity dates, adverse rulings) so counsel can file on day one of the response window. | counsel |
| Served | Proof of service in hand | Response deadline runs from service (state: typically around 30 days, federal around 21 days for a Rule 12 response; counsel confirms the exact date). Engage counsel immediately with the verdict table and outline. Insurer notice the same day if a policy exists. | counsel + insurer |
| Sender restricted by a court | Sender previously declared a vexatious litigant, or subject-matter/venue defects | Raise the restriction order and venue in the first response; cite the order by case number from your research. | counsel |

## Standing notes

- Registered-agent receipt is not service on the operating entity. Mail surfacing months late means the demand sat through a gap; check the docket for anything filed during it before assuming you are clean.
- Insurer notice deadlines commonly run from *awareness of circumstances*, not from filing. A demand letter can start that clock even with no suit pending.
- A settlement number is never a Deep decision alone: if counsel says the claim survives the demurrer stage, the figures reach Deep with counsel's cost-to-defend estimate beside them.
- Site hygiene fixes ship regardless of posture. They are the only lever that stops the next sender, and they are cheap next to any defense.
