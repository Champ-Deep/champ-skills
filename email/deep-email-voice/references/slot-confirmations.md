# Slot confirmations

Two lines each: greeting with the slot repeated in full, then the ask. The slot
always restates weekday, date, time and zone, because that is the only part the
reader has to act on.

## Confirm

```
Hi Ajay,

Does Monday 3 Nov, 8:30 PM IST work for you? Reply yes and I will send the invite.

Cheers,
Deep
```

## Move (when a dependency slipped)

```
Hi Ajay,

I need to push our sync to Tuesday 4 Nov, 8:30 PM IST. Does that work instead?

Cheers,
Deep
```

The moved-to slot comes first and the reason stays to one clause. A long
explanation invites a reply about the reason rather than about the time.

## Hold (when the slot is not final yet)

```
Hi Ajay,

I am confirming Monday 3 Nov, 8:30 PM IST for our sync. I will send the invite once
the calendar clears.

Cheers,
Deep
```

## Register

- Interns and juniors: keep `I hope you're doing well!` above the ask.
- Leaders, vendors, the Chief: straight to the slot, no warm line.
- External clients: brand voice of the sending company, per `deep-email-voice`
  section 1.

Never add a second ask, a recap of the thread, or new agenda items to a
confirmation: he answers "yes" or "move it" and stops, and anything else in the
message competes with the reply he needs to give.
