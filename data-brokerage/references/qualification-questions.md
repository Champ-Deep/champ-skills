# Qualification questions and draft skeletons

## Buyer questions (ask before any company is introduced)

1. Which operational datasets are actively procured, for what uses, sectors, geographies, and languages, and what are the minimum acceptance criteria?
2. Can an India-based corporate sourcing partner participate, and what agreement and payment requirements apply?
3. How are introductions registered and protected, including duplicates, existing accounts, related companies, and renewals?
4. What determines our fee, when is it earned and paid, and what cancellation, clawback, or exclusivity terms apply? Is the fee separate from the supplier's proceeds?
5. Can initial qualification use an aggregate inventory only, and who performs rights review, privacy review, secure preparation, and sample approval?
6. What licence terms will the supplier face: permitted AI uses, downstream recipients, duration, exclusivity, retention, deletion, and rights protection?

## Supplier questions (ask in the opt-in discovery thread)

1. Who owns each layer of the records, and do customer terms, employee or contractor agreements, and confidentiality clauses permit licensing them for AI training?
2. Which systems, fields, date ranges, and record counts exist, and is it real operational history rather than demos or synthetic data?
3. Do the records contain personal, sensitive, or client-confidential data, and what de-identification runs inside the owner's environment before anything leaves?
4. Is the material already public or easily replicated online, and what makes it non-replicable: domain depth, workflow detail, judgment calls?
5. Who is the single accountable owner who can approve packages, sign, and move reviews?
6. What commercial structure is acceptable: one-time or recurring, permitted uses, retention and deletion terms, and a separately disclosed originator fee?

## Draft skeletons

Buyer first touch, no company named yet:

> Subject: Operational data sourcing partnership
>
> Hi [Name], our team runs SME and sector-specific outreach alongside our business-data and enrichment services. We are exploring a sourcing partnership to identify companies whose operational records match current requirements. Could we discuss your active buying brief and corporate referral terms first? We would start from company-approved inventories of systems, record types, history, and rights status, without collecting raw data.

Supplier opt-in opener, separate thread, one CTA:

> Subject: Operational data records your company may be able to license
>
> Hi [Name], alongside our data and enrichment work we are assessing whether selected companies' operational records fit data-licensing opportunities. This is an inventory conversation only: which systems, what history, who holds licensing authority. No files or access are requested. Interested in a short call to see whether it is a fit?

Rules for both drafts: assert only what is already true; bracket every unknown; never imply a buyer is confirmed, a fee is agreed, a referral is registered, or a review is underway until it is.
