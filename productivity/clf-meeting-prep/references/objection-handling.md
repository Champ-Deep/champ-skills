# CLF Objection Handling Reference

Pre-written responses to the 12 most common objections across all four brands.
Use these verbatim or adapt to the pitch — always brand-specific where noted.

---

## HOW TO USE THIS FILE

When building the pitch, include a brief "Anticipated Objections" section in the appendix
OR weave the rebuttals into the relevant pitch sections (e.g., accuracy objection addressed
in Section 3, pricing objection addressed in Section 6).

For the NotebookLM deck, the objection block becomes speaker notes on the "Why Us" slide.

---

## OBJECTION 1 — "Your data is expensive."

**The underlying fear:** We'll pay more than the value we receive.

**Response:**
> "We understand that data investment requires clear ROI justification. The relevant
> comparison is not our price versus a competitor's list price — it's the cost per
> qualified conversation your team actually has. A cheaper database with 60% deliverability
> costs you more per connected call than a premium database with 95%+ deliverability.
> We're happy to model this with your current metrics."

**Brand-specific add:**
- LakeB2B: "Our 95%+ deliverability SLA means you pay for contacts that reach inboxes —
  not bounced addresses you'll never see converted."
- Ampliz: "At 99% accuracy, Ampliz eliminates the hidden cost of bad data: returned mail,
  wrong-number calls, and compliance risk."
- SGS: "Custom research means you pay for exactly what you need, nothing more. There's no
  licensing cost for records you'll never use."

---

## OBJECTION 2 — "We already have a data vendor / we use ZoomInfo / Cognism."

**The underlying fear:** Switching costs outweigh the benefit.

**Response:**
> "We're not asking you to switch — we're asking you to compare on a specific use case.
> Run [their current vendor] and [our brand] against the same ICP spec and measure
> deliverability, fill rate, and contact accuracy side by side. Most clients who do this
> pilot keep both for different use cases, or migrate fully within 90 days."

**Brand-specific add:**
- LakeB2B: "ZoomInfo and Cognism are strong in North America and Western Europe. LakeB2B's
  coverage advantage is in APAC, LATAM, and the Middle East — markets where their depth
  drops significantly."
- Ampliz: "No general B2B platform matches Ampliz in the healthcare vertical. The HCP
  data depth, NPI coverage, and HIPAA-aware handling are purpose-built — not an add-on."
- SGS: "SGS doesn't compete with database platforms. We fill the gaps they leave — custom
  research for niche verticals, append services for your existing CRM, and sourcing
  for the contacts no platform has indexed."

## OBJECTION 3 — "We have GDPR / compliance concerns."

**The underlying fear:** Legal liability from contact data.

**Response:**
> "Compliance is not a blocker — it's a selection criteria. Every record we deliver
> comes with a documented sourcing chain and compliant basis for outreach. We provide
> full compliance documentation with every dataset and are happy to involve your legal
> or data protection team in the review process before any data is transferred."

**Brand-specific add:**
- Ampliz: "Ampliz is ISO 27001 certified and operates with HIPAA-aware data handling
  protocols. For GDPR compliance, we provide consent documentation and sourcing
  records for every contact."
- SGS: "Part of SGS's value proposition is compliance architecture. Every custom-built
  list includes full chain-of-custody documentation for your DPO."

---

## OBJECTION 4 — "We're not sure about your data accuracy."

**The underlying fear:** We'll pay for stale or incorrect records.

**Response:**
> "We operate on a replacement SLA — not a promise. If delivered contacts don't meet
> our guaranteed accuracy threshold, we replace them at no additional cost. We're also
> happy to run a sample pull against your ICP spec before any commercial commitment,
> so you can validate accuracy on your own terms."

**Brand-specific add:**
- Ampliz: "Our 99% data accuracy guarantee is backed by a documented replacement policy.
  For healthcare contacts, we also include NPI verification status on every record."
- LakeB2B: "Our 95%+ email deliverability SLA is contractual — not a benchmark average.
  Under-delivery triggers automatic replacement."

---

## OBJECTION 5 — "We need to run this by procurement / legal / the CFO."

**The underlying fear:** Internal approval process will slow or kill the deal.

**Response:**
> "Completely expected — and we can help accelerate that process. We can provide a
> one-page summary formatted for internal approvals, a standard security and compliance
> questionnaire pre-filled with our certifications, and a pilot proposal at a threshold
> that typically sits below procurement review. What does your internal approval process
> typically require?"

---

## OBJECTION 6 — "We tried data vendors before and it didn't work."

**The underlying fear:** History of wasted investment; skepticism about data quality.

**Response:**
> "That's the most useful context you've given us. Tell us what failed — was it
> deliverability, ICP mismatch, data age, or something else? The answer tells us
> exactly what to solve for in this engagement. Most bad data experiences trace back
> to ICP-spec mismatches: buying a broad list when you needed a surgical one.
> Our process starts with your ICP before we pull a single record."

---

## OBJECTION 7 — "Can we do a trial / free sample first?"

**The underlying fear:** Want proof before investment.

**Response:**
> "Yes. This is exactly how we recommend starting. We'll pull a sample of [X] contacts
> matching your ICP spec — at no cost — and you test deliverability and relevance
> against your own criteria. If it meets your threshold, we proceed to a commercial
> agreement. If it doesn't, you've spent nothing."

**Note:** Position the sample as a standard first step, not a concession.

---

## OBJECTION 8 — "Your contract terms are too long / rigid."

**The underlying fear:** Lock-in without proven value.

**Response:**
> "We offer flexible engagement structures. Pilot programs are typically month-to-month.
> Standard engagements can be structured as one-time project, quarterly, or annual —
> with annual pricing carrying the strongest value. The goal is to earn your confidence
> before asking for a long-term commitment."

---

## OBJECTION 9 — "We don't have budget right now."

**The underlying fear:** Real budget constraint OR this is a polite no.

**Response:**
> "Understood. Two questions: Is this a timing issue for Q1/Q2 planning, or a genuine
> absence of data budget? If it's timing, we can hold pricing and structure a
> pre-commitment pilot that begins when budget opens. If it's a budget-build scenario,
> we can help you build the ROI case internally — we've done this for other teams
> justifying data investment for the first time."

---

## OBJECTION 10 — "We need references / case studies before we move forward."

**The underlying fear:** No proof of results for their specific vertical.

**Response:**
> "We can provide references from clients in your vertical under NDA. We can also
> share anonymized case studies with verified performance metrics. Which would be
> more useful for your internal stakeholders: a peer reference call, or a written
> case study they can review independently?"

---

## OBJECTION 11 — "Your coverage for [specific niche/geography] is weak."

**The underlying fear:** Our database isn't deep enough for their use case.

**Response:**
> "That's the right question to ask upfront. Let us run a live database count against
> your exact ICP spec — including the niche and geography — before any commitment.
> If our coverage doesn't meet your threshold, we'll tell you honestly. And in cases
> where our standard database has gaps, SGS custom research can build the missing
> segment from primary sources."

---

## OBJECTION 12 — "How fresh is your data? When was it last updated?"

**The underlying fear:** Stale data means wasted outreach.

**Response:**
> "Our data is refreshed on a rolling 90-day cycle — meaning any record you receive
> was verified within the last quarter. For contacts in high-churn roles (SDRs, BDRs,
> junior marketing), we apply additional freshness filters and flag records where
> tenure signals suggest potential movement. We also offer a post-delivery bounce
> replacement for any records that hard-bounce within 30 days."

**Brand-specific add:**
- Ampliz: "For HCP data, physician records are cross-referenced against NPI registry
  updates on a 60-day cycle — faster than any general B2B platform."
