## 1. LakeB2B {#lakeb2b}

### Core Identity
- **Tagline:** ENABLING GROWTH
- **Position:** Enterprise B2B data and marketing technology platform
- **Parent:** Champions Group
- **Primary buyer:** VP Marketing, Director of Demand Gen, Sales Operations, CRO

### Voice Attributes
- Formal and authoritative
- Data-volume and coverage-forward
- Lead with scale, back with accuracy
- Enterprise credibility before warmth

### Key Stats (use in pitch)
- 440M+ B2B contacts globally
- 36M+ companies tracked
- Data refreshed every 90 days
- 95%+ email deliverability SLA
- 40+ countries covered
- Verticals: SalesTech, MarTech, RecruitTech, GrowthTech

### Approved Opening Language
- "LakeB2B enables growth-stage and enterprise sales teams to reach decision-makers
  at scale with verified, compliant B2B contact data."
- "Our database of 440M+ contacts gives your sales team the depth to go wide or
  surgical — by industry, title, company size, technology stack, or geography."

### Approved "Why Us" Differentiators
1. Scale: 440M+ contacts vs. most providers at 100M-200M
2. Freshness: 90-day data refresh cycle
3. Deliverability SLA: 95%+ or we replace
4. Vertical depth: dedicated technology users lists, healthcare contacts, financial services
5. Custom builds: we pull to your ICP spec, not just filtered downloads

### Standard Proof Block (no case study provided)
> "A mid-market SaaS client targeting IT Directors in North America achieved a 4.2x
> increase in qualified pipeline within 90 days of deploying a LakeB2B technology users
> list — moving from 12% email deliverability on their existing vendor data to 96%
> verified deliverability on ours."

| Entry / Trial | Up to 5,000 contacts | Pilot pricing | 8 data fields, 72hr delivery, 10% replacement |
| Standard | 10K–100K contacts | Volume-tiered | 15 data fields, 48hr delivery, 15% replacement, account manager |

## 5. Multi-Brand Positioning {#multi-brand}

When pitching two or more brands together, use this framework:

### Lead Brand Selection Logic
- Prospect is in **sales/revenue** function → Lead with **LakeB2B** (volume + coverage)
- Prospect is in **marketing/demand gen** → Lead with **Ampliz** (intelligence + accuracy)
- Prospect is in **healthcare** vertical → Lead with **Ampliz Healthcare Intelligence**
- Prospect needs **custom research/enrichment** → Lead with **SGS**
- Prospect is a **startup or accelerator** → Lead with **Champions Accelerator**

### Ecosystem Bridge Language
> "What gives [Lead Brand] a structural advantage over standalone data vendors is the
> Champions Group ecosystem. Where [Lead Brand] covers [primary need], [Secondary Brand]
> adds [complementary capability] — giving you a single relationship with full-stack
> data and intelligence support."

### Multi-Brand Proof Statement
> "Champions Group portfolio companies, including LakeB2B, Ampliz, and Span Global Services,
> collectively serve 3,000+ enterprise clients across North America, EMEA, and APAC — giving
> our clients the flexibility to scale from a targeted pilot with one brand to an integrated
> data infrastructure across all three."
