# Pricing benchmarks for an outreach bid

Read this in step 5 of the build sequence. Every figure below was read off a vendor page or a market round-up in November 2026; re-check the number on the vendor's own page before it reaches the client, because these move. Secondary-source figures that could not be confirmed on the primary page get flagged in the note as an open check.

By deal size (Cleverly, March 2026, read via roimaxi.com): USD 150 to 400 for 5k to 20k deals; USD 400 to 800 for 20k to 100k; USD 800 to 1,500+ at 100k and up. Enterprise AI security sits in the top band, which is the fair-standard argument for a per-meeting fee above USD 300.

Wider published range across vendor guides: USD 50 to 5,000 per meeting; a typical B2B band of USD 80 to 250 (DevCommX); a market ceiling of `ACV x close rate x show rate` per booked meeting, with a quarter of it as a sane target price.

## Cost model shape

1. Tools per seat x seats, plus data and enrichment, plus labour (title and cost centre only).
2. Divide the monthly total by the volume target for cost per unit; compare against the anchor fee.
3. One-time setup = labour for ICP, list build, messaging, profile setup (about three weeks) plus the first month of tools during warm-up.
4. Convert INR labour at a stated FX rate and label the rate as an assumption.

## Anchor, floor, and the two structures

- Anchor: covers cost per unit with margin, sits inside the published band for the deal size.
- Floor: the walk-away, in the note but never in the reply.
- Second structure: same setup, lower fee, higher commission, so the client picks between two of ours.
- Add the protected terms next to the price: written definition of qualified, invoicing cycle, commission due when the client is paid, CRM read access, volume as a target from month two.
