# REACH: Activate (Stage A)

Stop sending one lonely email. One research session becomes up to seven ways to show up, all on one plan, so the prospect keeps encountering you on purpose.

## Tailor this first (edit once, then save your copy)

```
WHAT WE SELL: [one line]
CHANNELS I CAN USE: [email, LinkedIn, phone/voice, content, ads, referral]
ASSET TOOLS I HAVE: [NotebookLM, Google Slides, Canva, Gamma, none]
BRAND VOICE + COLORS: [e.g. "Champions Orange #F26722 and white, warm and confident"]
```

## When to use

A brief and ideally a wedge email exist, and the account is worth more than a single touch. Trigger after Engage.

## The workflow

1. Take the brief or dossier. Keep one core wedge consistent across every piece.
2. Produce all of these, each ready to use:
   - EMAIL (reuse the wedge email or write it).
   - LINKEDIN ENGAGE-FIRST: 2 comment angles on their recent posts, plus a connection note.
   - LINKEDIN DM for after they accept.
   - VOICE NOTE / CALL OPENER: a 20-second spoken script.
   - ONE-PAGE ASSET OUTLINE: the 5 sections of a battle card or mini-deck tailored to their wedge.
   - CONTENT HOOK: one LinkedIn post idea that stops this buyer scrolling.
   - SEQUENCE LOGIC: which channel leads and the order over the first 10 days.
3. If the rep wants a deck: produce a slide-by-slide outline (title, 3 to 5 bullets, speaker note, visual cue) they can paste into NotebookLM, Google Slides, or Gamma, applying BRAND VOICE + COLORS.

## Output

A coordinated multi-channel kit for one account, every piece consistent with the same wedge. If asked, deliver just one channel at a time.

## Power-ups (optional)

- Skills: `prospect-deck`, `prospect-campaign-plan`, `power-design` and `pptx` for decks, `visual-report-builder` for one-pagers, `canvas-design` for visuals, `b2b-blog-writer` for content.
- Tools: NotebookLM for grounded deck building, Canva for polish.
- Champ stack: ChampVoice for the voice touch, SlideSmith for decks.
