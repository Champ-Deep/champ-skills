# Pitch Matrix: the 14 audience categories

Source: Champions Infratech Audience Pitch Matrix, supplied by Sreedeep Surapaneni. Column meanings: the pitch is what the sender says, "why it works" is for the sender only and is never read aloud, and the ask is a single next step.

Priority coding. 1-Core: direct revenue or capital. 2-High: high-leverage influence over deals. 3-Enable: supply and delivery capability. 4-Support: brand and demand support. 5-Qualify: establish fit first.

## 1. Builder / Developer
**Priority:** 1 - Core

**Who they are.** Landowners and residential/township developers seeking a differentiator on large land parcels.

**What they actually want.** Faster sales velocity, higher price per sq ft, a reason buyers choose them over the project next door.

**The pitch (say this).** We turn your land into a beachfront address. Champions Infratech designs and builds a Crystal Lagoons man-made beach at the heart of your project, so every unit sells as waterfront. Same land, a category of its own.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Their pain is undifferentiated inventory. A beach is the only amenity a competitor cannot copy cheaply, and it re-rates the whole masterplan, not one tower.

**The ask / next step.** Share your land parcel size and location. We return an indicative lagoon footprint and a price-uplift view in one week.

## 2. Finance Consultant
**Priority:** 2 - High

**Who they are.** Debt and structured finance advisors placing project funding for developers.

**What they actually want.** Bankable projects that close, and mandates that repeat.

**The pitch (say this).** Lagoon-anchored projects carry stronger pre-sales and premium realisation, which strengthens the very metrics your lenders underwrite. We bring you the asset story; you bring the structure.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** They are judged on deal quality, not deal count. A differentiated asset de-risks their placement and makes them look sharp to their lender panel.

**The ask / next step.** Introduce us to two developer clients with land banked and stalled positioning. Referral economics agreed upfront.

## 3. Investor (VC / PE / Family Office / Fund)
**Priority:** 1 - Core

**Who they are.** Capital allocators seeking real-asset exposure with yield and appreciation in India.

**What they actually want.** Downside protection, a defensible moat, and a clear exit.

**The pitch (say this).** Two ways in. Invest at project level alongside the developer, or invest in the lagoon infrastructure itself and earn from the uplift it creates across the masterplan. The technology licence is the moat.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Real assets with an exclusive licence attached is a rare combination. It answers the first question every allocator asks: what stops someone replicating this.

**The ask / next step.** Share your ticket size and hold horizon. We will map you to project-level or platform-level entry.

## 4. Construction Company
**Priority:** 3 - Enable

**Who they are.** Civil contractors and general construction firms executing large residential and mixed-use builds.

**What they actually want.** Order book, technically interesting scope, long project runway.

**The pitch (say this).** Lagoon-anchored masterplans are large, multi-phase and specification-heavy. We need construction partners who can execute to the standard the technology demands.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** This is scope, not a sales pitch. Lead with the work available and the qualification bar.

**The ask / next step.** Send capability deck and three comparable completed projects for empanelment.

## 5. Product Company
**Priority:** 3 - Enable

**Who they are.** Suppliers of filtration, pumps, decking, water systems, lighting, marine and landscape products.

**What they actually want.** Specification into a repeatable pipeline rather than one-off orders.

**The pitch (say this).** Every lagoon community carries a standard product stack. Get specified once and you are in every project we build, across India.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Specification lock-in is worth far more to them than any single purchase order. That is the hook.

**The ask / next step.** Send product catalogue and India service footprint for the approved vendor list.

## 6. Architect
**Priority:** 2 - High

**Who they are.** Masterplanning and design practices working on township, resort and mixed-use schemes.

**What they actually want.** Signature projects, creative freedom, work worth publishing.

**The pitch (say this).** A lagoon reorganises an entire masterplan. Frontage multiplies, circulation changes, the whole scheme gains a centre. This is the most interesting brief on your desk this year.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Architects respond to design opportunity before commercial terms. Lead with the brief, not the economics.

**The ask / next step.** Half day working session on one of your live schemes to show what a lagoon does to the plan.

## 7. Interior Designer
**Priority:** 4 - Support

**Who they are.** Residential and hospitality interiors practices.

**What they actually want.** Premium-budget projects with a coherent design language and volume.

**The pitch (say this).** Waterfront living carries a premium interior expectation, across hundreds of units and hospitality keys. Recurring scope, consistent brief, one relationship.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Volume with a single coherent brief is unusually attractive. Emphasise repeatability across phases.

**The ask / next step.** Portfolio review and a discussion on show-unit scope for the next phase.

## 8. EPC Consultant
**Priority:** 2 - High

**Who they are.** Engineering, procurement and construction consultants advising owners on delivery.

**What they actually want.** Technically credible partners who will not embarrass them in front of their client.

**The pitch (say this).** We hold the technology licence and the delivery method. You hold the client relationship and the owner's engineer role. Clean division, no overlap.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** They fear reputational risk more than they want margin. Lead with licence, method and proof of delivery.

**The ask / next step.** Technical session on the water system, treatment cycle and delivery programme.

## 9. Media & Magazine
**Priority:** 4 - Support

**Who they are.** Property, design, luxury lifestyle and business publications.

**What they actually want.** A story nobody else is running.

**The pitch (say this).** India's first generation of man-made beach communities is being built now. We will give you access to the technology, the sites and the numbers, ahead of anyone else.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Media trade in exclusivity and novelty. Offer both. Do not offer an advertorial first.

**The ask / next step.** Site visit and founder interview for a first-look feature.

## 10. Real Estate Agent / Consultant
**Priority:** 1 - Core

**Who they are.** Brokerages, channel partners and advisory firms with developer and landlord relationships.

**What they actually want.** Inventory that moves fast and commissions that follow.

**The pitch (say this).** Waterfront inventory sells at a premium and sells quicker. Bring us a landlord or developer with the right parcel and you are in the deal on both sides.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Their currency is velocity and commission. Say both plainly, in the first line.

**The ask / next step.** Referral agreement, then walk us through your top three land or developer relationships.

## 11. Hospitality
**Priority:** 2 - High

**Who they are.** Hotel operators, resort brands, F&B and leisure operators.

**What they actually want.** Footfall, year-round occupancy and a location that does not depend on a coastline.

**The pitch (say this).** A beach without the coast. Build a resort anywhere in India, inland, and still sell a waterfront stay. Beach clubs, water sports and F&B run all year.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Coastal land is scarce, expensive and regulated. This removes the single hardest constraint in their site selection.

**The ask / next step.** Explore an operator tie-up on a live lagoon masterplan.

## 12. Longevity
**Priority:** 3 - Enable

**Who they are.** Longevity clinics, medical wellness operators and preventive health ventures.

**What they actually want.** A physical setting that makes the proposition credible and residential demand that sustains it.

**The pitch (say this).** Longevity needs a place, not just a protocol. Water, light, movement and open space, designed in, with a resident population already living there.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** The environment is their product's delivery mechanism. Frame the community as infrastructure for their programme.

**The ask / next step.** Concept discussion on a longevity anchor within a lagoon community.

## 13. Wellness
**Priority:** 3 - Enable

**Who they are.** Spa, fitness, yoga, retreat and wellness hospitality operators.

**What they actually want.** Destination-quality settings with recurring resident and visitor demand.

**The pitch (say this).** Beach, water sports, open-water swimming and waterfront studios, in a community where members already live. Destination quality, daily footfall.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** Most wellness operators choose between destination appeal and consistent volume. This gives both.

**The ask / next step.** Site walk and a conversation on wellness scope in the next phase.

## 14. Others
**Priority:** 5 - Qualify

**Who they are.** Government bodies, education, retail, sports academies, tech and anyone with land or footfall to contribute.

**What they actually want.** Varies. Establish the interest before pitching anything.

**The pitch (say this).** We build man-made beach communities across India on licensed Crystal Lagoons technology. Tell me what you do and I will tell you honestly whether there is a fit.

**Why it works on them (sender only, never read aloud).** An honest qualifying question outperforms a generic pitch and protects the brand from a poor fit.

**The ask / next step.** Fifteen minute qualification call before anything is sent.
