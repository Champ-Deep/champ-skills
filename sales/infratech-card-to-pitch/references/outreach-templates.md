# Outreach templates

All four assets are written for the person who is actually sending, normally Champ, following up on the chairman's conversation. If the chairman is sending, switch to first person and drop the "on behalf of" line.

## Email

**Shape.** Subject, then 90 to 130 words in four moves.

1. Anchor: the event, the chairman, the conversation. One line, specific enough to be remembered.
2. Bridge: what Champions Infratech does, in one sentence, angled at their category.
3. Offer: the category-specific value, referencing something real about their business.
4. Ask: one next step, with the scheduler link.

**Rules.** No apology for the delay. No "I hope this finds you well". No jargon. No bullet points unless three genuinely parallel items exist. No attachment on the first email unless the category asks for one. Sign-off carries name, title, Champions Infratech, and the scheduler link.

**Subject lines.** Give three. One event-anchored, one outcome-anchored, one curiosity-anchored. Keep all three under 60 characters.

## LinkedIn connection note

Under 300 characters. Reference the event and the chairman by name, one clause on why you are connecting, no link, no ask beyond the connection itself. The pitch happens after they accept.

## WhatsApp nudge

Two lines, sent five to seven days after the email. Line one re-anchors the event in under twelve words. Line two is the ask, restated smaller than the email ask: a fifteen minute call rather than a session.

## Call opener

Three sentences for when they answer, then two questions. The questions come before any pitch. For a developer: parcel size and current positioning problem. For an investor: ticket size and hold horizon. For a consultant: which of their clients has land banked and stalled. Ask, listen, then pitch to what they said.

## Worked example 1: Builder / Developer

**Contact:** VP Projects at a mid-size township developer, met at a real estate summit.

**Subject options**
- Following up from the [Event] floor
- Turning your [City] parcel into waterfront
- The one amenity your competitor cannot copy

**Body**

Mr [Name],

Our chairman, Subhakar Rao, met you at [Event] last week and mentioned your [City] township. Champions Infratech builds man-made beach communities in India on licensed Crystal Lagoons technology.

In practice that means a beach at the centre of your masterplan, so units across the scheme sell as waterfront rather than one premium tower carrying the whole project. It works inland, with no coastline required.

If you share the parcel size and location, we will come back within a week with an indicative lagoon footprint and what it does to your frontage.

Fifteen minutes this week: [scheduler link]

[Name]
[Title], Champions Infratech

**LinkedIn note**

Mr [Name], you met our chairman Subhakar Rao at [Event]. I look after Champions Infratech, we build man-made beach communities anchored on Crystal Lagoons technology. Would like to stay connected on the [City] scheme.

**WhatsApp nudge**

Mr [Name], following up from [Event], where you met our chairman.
Would a fifteen minute call this week work to look at the lagoon footprint for your [City] parcel?

## Worked example 2: Investor (family office)

**Contact:** Principal at a single-family office, met at a family office forum.

**Subject options**
- From the [Forum] session on real assets
- Real asset exposure with a licence attached
- Two ways into India beach communities

**Body**

[Name],

Our chairman, Subhakar Rao, met you at [Forum] and the conversation turned to real-asset allocation in India.

Champions Infratech builds man-made beach communities anchored on licensed Crystal Lagoons technology. There are two entry routes: at project level alongside the developer, or into the lagoon infrastructure itself, which earns from the uplift it creates across the masterplan.

The licence is the part worth twenty minutes of your time, since it answers the replication question before it gets asked.

If you share ticket size and hold horizon, we will map you to the right entry point rather than send a generic deck.

[scheduler link]

[Name]
[Title], Champions Infratech

**Note for the sender.** Confirm the actual available structure and the licence terms with the user before this one goes out. An allocator will ask for terms in the first meeting and a vague answer costs the relationship.
