# Champions Infratech: positioning and claim boundaries

## The story in one paragraph

Champions Infratech designs and builds man-made beach communities across India, using licensed Crystal Lagoons water technology as the anchor amenity. The lagoon turns an ordinary land parcel into a waterfront address, which re-rates an entire masterplan rather than a single tower. It applies to residential townships, mixed-use schemes, resorts and hospitality assets, and it removes the hardest constraint in Indian coastal development: the coast itself. Champions Infratech sits within Champions Group, a Bangalore-based multi-venture holding company.

## The three commercial routes

1. **Build.** A developer or landowner brings a parcel. Champions Infratech designs and delivers the lagoon and the beach-anchored masterplan around it.
2. **Invest.** Capital enters either at project level alongside the developer, or at the level of the lagoon infrastructure itself, earning from the uplift it creates across the masterplan.
3. **Partner and refer.** Consultants, brokers, finance advisors and agencies introduce their own landlord, builder and real estate investment firm relationships, on agreed referral economics.

## What may be said freely

- Champions Infratech builds man-made beach communities in India, anchored on Crystal Lagoons technology.
- It holds a licence for that technology.
- A lagoon creates waterfront frontage across a masterplan and works inland, away from any coastline.
- Both project-level and infrastructure-level investment routes exist as options.
- Partners can participate through referral arrangements.

## What must be verified before it goes in writing to an outsider

These are open items. Do not resolve them by inference, and do not let a confident sentence paper over them.

- **Licence terms.** Territory, exclusivity and duration of the Crystal Lagoons licence. Matters most for the Investor and EPC Consultant categories, where the first question will be "what stops someone replicating this".
- **Investment structure.** The dual entry route is described as an option. Confirm what is actually available before an investor asks for terms in the first meeting, because they will.
- **Referral economics.** Both the Finance Consultant and Real Estate Agent pitches reference referral terms. Agree them internally before going out, or the first good referral stalls.
- **Any number at all.** No price uplift percentage, lagoon cost, IRR, sales velocity figure or completed-project count has been supplied. Do not improvise one. A pitch with no numbers is weaker than a pitch with verified numbers and far stronger than a pitch with invented ones.

When a category pitch needs a number to land, write the sentence so the number can be dropped in later, and tell the user in the brief which figure would strengthen it most.

## Reference points that already exist in the group

Champions Ranch is a Champions Infratech property and can be used as a credibility and hospitality reference where relevant. Confirm current status with the user before describing it in detail to an external contact.

## Tone

Plain, direct, commercially literate. The reader is a developer, an allocator or a principal who has heard every amenity pitch in the market. Lead with what changes for them, not with what the technology is. Technology is the proof, not the opening.
