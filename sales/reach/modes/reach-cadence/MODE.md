# REACH: Cadence (Stage C)

Most reps quit after touch two. Buyers reply at touch five. The deal was never lost, the follow-up was. This builds the whole sequence so nothing falls through the cracks.

## Tailor this first (edit once, then save your copy)

```
WHAT WE SELL: [one line]
DEFAULT CHANNELS: [email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, phone]
BRAND VOICE: [e.g. "warm, useful, never pushy, no em-dashes"]
VALUE ASSETS I CAN SHARE: [case study, data point, article, free resource]
```

## When to use

A first touch has gone out, or a lead has gone quiet. Trigger after Engage or Activate. The timeline resets the moment the prospect replies.

## The workflow

1. Take the prospect brief and the first touch (or ask for them).
2. Write a touch for each day. For each: the goal, the channel, and the message within these limits.
   - DAY 1, Initial context: under 100 words, one CTA, curious not pushy.
   - DAY 3, Add value: under 120 words, share one useful resource or insight.
   - DAY 7, Bridge to conversation: under 100 words, a genuine question, no pitch.
   - DAY 14, Strategic pivot: under 130 words, a new angle or a different person to talk to.
   - DAY 21, Last warm touch: under 120 words, give them control ("reply PAUSE if you'd rather I stop").
   - DAY 30, Archive or nurture: a short decision message.
3. Vary the angle every touch. Never "just bumping this to the top of your inbox."
4. Offer to adapt the cadence for a different channel mix if the rep names one.

## Output

A six-touch sequence the rep can queue, each touch distinct and on-voice.

## Power-ups (optional)

- Skills: `outbound-email` (sequence mode), `marketing:email-sequence`.
- Connectors: Gmail or MS365 to queue, HubSpot to log.
- Champ stack: Champmail to execute the cadence and detect replies.
