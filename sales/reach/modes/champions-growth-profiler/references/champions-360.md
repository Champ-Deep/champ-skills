# Champions 360 lens

The shared rule for every skill that judges a company or a person on Champions Group's behalf: prospect research, meeting prep, social triage, outreach sequences, deck and showcase builds. Load it beside `champions-brands.md`.

**The failure this fixes:** every account got read as "will they buy LakeB2B data". A Group of 12 companies has at least ten ways to relate to one company. The lens scores all of them, picks ONE for the first touch, and holds the rest for Deep.

## 1. Relationship modes

Score every account on each row: `strong`, `possible`, `none`. One line of evidence per non-`none` row.

| Mode | The question to ask | Champions asset that plays | Who decides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sell to** | What do they buy that we sell? | Any brand in `champions-brands.md` | Rep |
| **Buy from** | Do they sell something our 12 companies already pay for or need? | Group procurement, Cirralogix (cloud/IT), IP Momentum (telecom), Infometrics (ops) | Rep opens, Deep approves spend |
| **Partner or channel** | Could they resell us, or we them, to a customer base neither reaches alone? | LakeB2B white-label platform, SPAN, Ampliz self-serve, agency reseller model (Black & Beige pattern) | Deep |
| **Barter** | What do they have in surplus that we need, and the reverse? | Data for distribution, seats on Waves for speaker slots, Event Scout capture for attendee rights, Ranch or Club venue for sponsorship | Deep |
| **Co-create** | Is there a dataset, product, report or event neither side has that both could sell? | LakeB2B + their vertical data, Harbinger signals + their product signals, co-branded benchmark report | Deep |
| **Co-market** | Can we share an audience without money moving? | Joint webinar, Growth Meetup on Waves co-host, guest in each other's newsletter, case study swap | Rep proposes, Deep approves |
| **Invest or accelerate** | Early stage, raising, or with a venture arm? | Champions Accelerator (pre-seed, GTM mentorship) | Deep |
| **Host** | Do they need venues, offsites, incentive travel or executive hospitality? | Champions Club, Ranch, Yacht Club, Waves | Rep |
| **Hire or staff** | Are they hiring SDRs, data teams or ops roles, or shedding them? | Recruit Champ (ATS + placement), LakeB2B RecruitTech | Rep |
| **Land and assets** | Do they hold land, hospitality assets or township plans in India? | Champion Infratech (honesty guardrail: announced vision, not delivered) | Deep |
| **Learn or benchmark** | Are they a competitor, upstream vendor or a model we should study? | `competitive-product-teardown`, `competitive-research-verification` | Main lane |
| **Acquire or be acquired** | Distressed, sub-scale, or strategically adjacent? | Flag only. Never pitch. | Deep only |

## 2. Picking the opener

1. Lead with the mode closest to **their** KPI this quarter, not ours. A peer vendor gets a partner or barter opener, never a cold data pitch: the pitch they get from six other vendors a day is the one they ignore.
2. "Buy from" is a warm door. If they sell a tool one of our 12 companies would plausibly use, open as a buyer, sell second. The meeting books faster and the power balance is right.
3. One primary mode for the first touch. Up to two **held angles** written down for Deep. A sequence that pitches three relationships reads as spray.
4. Sister-brand collision: before any outreach, check `Atlas/Clients/`, the CRM and Harbinger's `contact_claims` for an existing relationship with any Champions company. An active client of SPAN is not a cold prospect for LakeB2B, it is an expansion conversation owned by whoever holds the account.
5. Barter, co-create, invest, land and acquire are **proposals**, never promises. The agent surfaces them in a `Held for Deep` block with the one-line case and the one thing Deep must decide. Nobody but Deep commits the Group to a non-cash relationship.
6. Keep the honesty guardrails from `champions-brands.md`: Lagoons and Smart Beach Cities are announced vision, numbers carry sources, unverified stays labelled.

## 3. Idea bank

Starting points, not a menu. Use one when the account's evidence supports it, and write new ones here when a real account produces one that worked.

| # | Play | When it fits | What we give | What we get |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Data for distribution** | A SaaS vendor with an installed base in our ICP | Enriched contact or intent data inside their product | Named as their recommended data partner, in-app and in docs |
| 2 | **Co-owned vertical dataset** | Industry association, vertical SaaS or publisher sitting on signals we lack | Build, QA and the sales motion | Joint ownership, joint price list, their logo on the proof |
| 3 | **Signal exchange** | A product company whose usage events reveal buying intent | Harbinger signals and enrichment | Their first-party signals feed our scoring, both sides label the source |
| 4 | **Waves seat instead of a discount** | Senior prospect asks for a price cut | A co-host slot or guest seats at a Growth Meetup on Waves | Full price, plus their top accounts in the room with ours |
| 5 | **Event Scout at their conference** | They run events and lose the business cards | Card capture, OCR, same-day follow-up list | Rights to the attendee list for our enrichment pool |
| 6 | **Accelerator track** | A prospect with a venture arm, portfolio or an internal spin-out | Champions Accelerator mentorship and GTM | First right to data and GTM services across the portfolio |
| 7 | **Talent plus data retainer** | They are standing up an SDR or research team | Recruit Champ staffs it, LakeB2B supplies the data they work | Pay on placement plus a data retainer, one invoice |
| 8 | **Reference deployment** | Cloud, security or IT vendor wanting a reference in India | Cirralogix or a Group company as a named deployment and case study | Preferential pricing across the 12 companies |
| 9 | **Co-branded benchmark report** | They have an expert or a dataset, we have the audience | Our data and distribution | Their expert, their list, shared leads with a written split |
| 10 | **White-label for their SMB base** | Agency, bank, chamber or marketplace serving small businesses | LakeB2B platform under their brand | Wholesale revenue and their long tail |
| 11 | **Reciprocal referral with fee** | Creative, PR, dev or consulting agency in our ICP | We refer creative and build work | They resell data and campaigns, fee on both sides in writing |
| 12 | **Wellness pilot for logo** | India-headquartered company with 200+ staff | Champ.fit, Health.fit or Food.fit pilot for employees | Logo, testimonial, a warm CXO relationship for the data side |
| 13 | **Calling bundle** | Outbound sales team paying retail for telephony | IP Momentum VoIP bundled with LakeB2B contacts | Stickier account, two Group companies on one contract |
| 14 | **Pilot for proof** | Buyer wants proof before budget | A 30-day scoped pilot | A published case study and three named introductions, agreed up front |
| 15 | **Open as a buyer** | They sell a tool our teams would plausibly use | A real evaluation by the relevant company | A warm meeting where we are the customer, then the reverse conversation |
| 16 | **Offsite swap** | A company whose product our teams want to trial | Ranch or Club offsite venue | A paid trial or a credit, plus their leadership on our turf for a day |
| 17 | **CSR or education joint program** | Companies with an India CSR mandate | Champions Education Trust as a delivery partner | Relationship with the leadership that controls far bigger budgets |
| 18 | **Marketplace listing** | They run an app or partner marketplace | A LakeB2B, Ampliz or Harbinger connector | Distribution and co-selling with their partner team |

## 4. Output block (paste into any profile, prep or triage note)

```
## 360 lens
Primary mode: <mode> via <Champions company>. Why: <one line of evidence>.
Held for Deep: <mode>: <one-line case>. Decide: <the one question>.
Held for Deep: <mode>: <one-line case>. Decide: <the one question>.
Collision check: <none found | existing relationship with X, owner Y>.
Scored none: <modes dismissed, no detail>.
```

Social triage uses the short form on each pipeline row: `360: <mode> / <company>`. A vendor pitch in the DM inbox is a `Buy from` or `Barter` candidate, not noise.
