# Champions Group Brand Reference

This is the brand-matching knowledge base for the growth profiler. Use it to pick the right Champions Group brand(s) for a prospect and to write accurate, credible pitch angles. Facts here were researched from public sources (July 2026); if a prospect conversation reveals newer internal positioning, the internal material wins.

**Group framing:** Champions Group is a Bangalore-headquartered conglomerate (corporate HQ listing at Marina Bay Financial Centre, Singapore; presence in USA, UK, Dubai, Japan, China) spanning data & growth marketing, real estate/infra, hospitality & luxury experiences, aviation, and venture acceleration. Chairman/founder: Subhakar Rao Surapaneni. Sreedeep Surapaneni is CEO of Champions Accelerator.

**Do not confuse** with "Champions Group Holdings" (championsgroupholdings.com), an unrelated US home-services/HVAC company.

---

## Tier 1 brands (primary recommendation targets)

### LakeB2B — data-driven growth & GTM
- **What it sells:** B2B data and growth-marketing services in four stacks — SalesTech (B2B + healthcare lead databases, prospecting), MarTech (campaign management, email/ABM/demand gen), RecruitTech (recruitment SaaS), GrowthTech (growth advisory, CRM+, expansion services). Also data enrichment, custom datasets, intent data (18,000+ intent topics), executive events (roundtables/webinars), and white-label platform offerings — "28 solutions."
- **Ideal customer:** VP/Director of Marketing, Demand Gen, Sales/RevOps, CROs, and founders at mid-market and enterprise firms. Strongest vertical: healthcare (providers, pharma, medical devices); also technology, education, automotive, manufacturing, oil & gas.
- **Proof points:** 7,000+ clients worldwide; ~1,800 new customers/year; operations in 6 countries (USA, India, UK, UAE, Singapore, Japan); client-reported "7X ROI increase."
- **Differentiators:** full-stack data + execution under one roof; healthcare data depth; access to the group's experiential ABM motion (Growth Meetups on Waves).
- **Recommend when:** the prospect has pipeline/ARR/GTM pain — thin pipeline, poor data quality, new-market expansion, ABM ambitions, event-led demand gen, or a sales team starved of accurate contacts/intent signals.

### Champion Lagoons / Champion Infratech — lagoon-led real estate
- **What it sells:** Lagoon-anchored real estate development. Champion Infratech is Champions Group's real estate division and the official associate of Crystal Lagoons® in India — man-made crystalline lagoons as anchor amenities that turn inland land into "beachfront" destinations.
- **Ideal customer:** real estate developers seeking differentiation, hospitality/resort operators, land owners with idle parcels, institutional investors, and public-sector bodies (public-access lagoon model).
- **Proof points:** closed-circuit water tech using far less water/energy than golf courses or pools; minimum viable lagoon ~2.4 acres; monetization via ticketed public access plus real-estate value uplift. Announced projects: Champions Beach Valley (Hosur, 100 acres) and Champions Beach City (Sarjapur, 25 acres) with combined ₹9,000 crore stated revenue potential; beach club projects announced for Ahmedabad and Jaipur; 2023 vision of "100 beaches and lagoons near every Indian city." MD: Hemamalini Nidamanuri.
- **Honesty guardrail:** public coverage is largely company press releases; there are no independently verified *completed* lagoon projects. Pitch as "announced vision backed by the Crystal Lagoons association," never as delivered infrastructure.
- **Recommend when:** the prospect owns or controls land, sells real estate that needs differentiation, runs hospitality assets with occupancy/footfall pain, or wants a marquee amenity to lift price-per-sqft.

### Champion Smart Beach Cities — smart-city / urban infra plays
- **What it is:** concept combining lagoon townships with digital infrastructure. Feb 2025 announcement: 10 Smart Beach Lagoon Cities across Karnataka with edge data centers (10MW total) — 5 around Bengaluru (Sarjapur, Electronic City, Hosur, Devanahalli, Marathahalli) and 5 in tier-2 cities (Mysore, Tumkuru, Raichur, Hubli, Mangaluru). Pitched features: AI-driven security, solar, wellness facilities, helipads, smart transport, lagoon-cooled data centers.
- **Ideal customer:** state governments and investment agencies, data-center/cloud tenants, land partners, HNI/NRI property buyers, infra investors.
- **Honesty guardrail:** concept-stage; announced via company press releases with no construction milestones or independent reporting. Frame as "announced vision," and lean on the edge-data-center angle for tech prospects.
- **Recommend when:** the prospect is in government/urban development, data centers/cloud infra, or large-scale township investment.

### Champions Club — CXO networking & luxury experiences
- **What it sells:** luxury lifestyle + executive membership club: yacht charters (Goa, Dubai, Bali), private jet charter/fractional ownership, helicopters, heritage villas/beach/farm stays, destination weddings and corporate events, VVIP concierge. Tiered Elite and Corporate memberships; also runs CXO community chapters.
- **Ideal customer:** CXOs, entrepreneurs, industrialists, HNIs; corporates buying offsites, incentive experiences, and client-hosting venues.
- **Proof points:** 10+ years operating; owns assets (yachts/properties) rather than purely brokering; hubs in Goa, Dubai, Bali, Bangalore.
- **Differentiator:** the experiential-networking layer of the group — the natural venue for relationship-led selling and executive community building.
- **Recommend when:** the person is a senior executive worth cultivating (relationship play), or the company needs premium client hosting, incentive travel, or executive-community access.

## Tier 2 brands (mention when the fit is specific)

- **Champions Accelerator** — pre-seed funding + mentorship for early-stage tech startups; CEO Sreedeep Surapaneni; also fronts the edge-data-center agenda. Fit: early-stage founders raising or seeking GTM mentorship.
- **Champions Ranch** (championsranch.farm) — operating eco-farm retreat near Bangalore (Hosur/Krishnagiri): horse riding, yoga, wellness therapies, farm-to-table dining, team-building, event spaces. Fit: corporate offsites, leadership retreats, reward trips.
- **Ampliz** — B2B & healthcare data-intelligence SaaS (APAC/US healthcare datasets). Fit: prospects wanting self-serve data tooling rather than managed services.
- **Span Global Services** — data-driven marketing and B2B data services; sister brand to LakeB2B.
- **MedicoReach** — healthcare-specialist contact data (physicians, executives, payers, telemedicine).
- **Others (one-liners, use sparingly):** Champions Infometrics (data operations/delivery), Champion Prefabs (prefabricated construction), Champions Yacht Club / Champion Yachts Dubai / Champions Tourism (maritime & travel), Champion Aviation (private aviation), Champion Superior Capital Solutions (financial services), Cirrologix (cloud/IT), IP Momentum (VoIP/telecom), Namma Cargo (logistics), ChampFit/FoodFit (fitness & nutrition), Champions Education Trust (CSR).

## Signature plays (use as CTAs and pitch hooks)

- **B2B Growth Meetups on Waves** (LakeB2B × Champions Club): luxury-yacht executive networking with data-driven pre-event outreach; formats from 25-person Singapore lunches to ~100-attendee Dubai gatherings; run in Dubai, Goa/Mumbai, Singapore, San Francisco, Saudi Arabia, Spain, London, Miami. Self-reported results: 2x conversion rates, ~40% shorter sales cycles, 3x deal sizes. The go-to CTA for senior prospects: "invite to the next Growth Meetup on Waves."
- **Global Investors Growth Summit (GIGS)**: group-run investor summit with a Champion Lagoons showcase. Fit: investors and capital-raising prospects. Public detail is sparse.
- **LakeB2B signal scan**: a low-friction first step for GTM prospects — run a data/intent scan on their ICP before proposing a program.

## Cross-brand selling logic

The group's strongest pitch is the *combination*: data-led targeting (LakeB2B/Ampliz) + experiential access (Champions Club/Waves) + asset plays (Lagoons/Smart Beach Cities). When a prospect fits two brands, lead with the one closest to their KPI and use the second as the differentiator ("no other data vendor can host your top 20 accounts on a yacht").

Typical pairings: real-estate developer → Lagoons (lead) + LakeB2B for HNI-buyer targeting; SaaS CRO → LakeB2B (lead) + Champions Club/Waves for exec-level ABM; government/infra official → Smart Beach Cities (lead) + GIGS for investor connect; healthcare marketer → LakeB2B or MedicoReach (lead) + Waves for KOL engagement; startup founder → Champions Accelerator (lead) + LakeB2B GrowthTech.
