# Cold Email Benchmarks (2026 study + Edward Sturm Short)

Source chain: analysis of 6,337 cold emails sent by 200+ students (Nov 2025 to May 2026), posted to r/internships on 2026-05-14 (reddit.com/r/internships/comments/1tcri9x), full breakdown at whali.co.uk/blog/cold-email-data-study-students-2026. Popularized in the YouTube Short youtube.com/shorts/zF_XIBi0SJM (Edward Sturm, Oct 2026).

## Read the caveat first

The dataset is student internship outreach, not B2B sales email. Treat every number here as directional. Where a number conflicts with the CLF team's own three-month field test (the eight email rules in SKILL.md), the field test wins. This study tightens targets, it does not override proven practice.

## Body length drives reply rate

| First-touch length | Reply rate |
|---|---|
| Under 100 words | 11.9% |
| 100 to 200 words | 1.9% |
| 200 to 300 words | 0.3% |

The 6x gap between under-100 and 200-300 words is the headline finding. It matches the CLF field test direction (shorter wins) but argues for a tighter target than the old 100-to-125 ideal.

## Subject lines drive open rate

| Subject pattern | Open rate |
|---|---|
| Under 30 characters | 83.7% |
| 70+ characters | 69.5% |
| Contains a number | 80.6% (highest pattern in the study) |
| Contains "interested in" | 79.0% |

One-word subjects get the best open rates per the First 1000 newsletter (cited by Sturm, not part of this dataset). Open rates are inflated 30 to 40% industry-wide by Apple Mail Privacy Protection auto-opens, so read all open-rate gaps as relative, not absolute.

## Follow-ups: peak at two, then it turns hostile

- One follow-up lifts reply rates 60%+ over no follow-up.
- Reply rate peaks at two follow-ups (three emails total).
- Past two follow-ups, replies drop and unsubscribes spike.
- Sturm's framing of the same data: 4.1% replies after one follow-up, 6.6% after two. Past that, you are spamming.

## Send timing (study, UTC)

Wednesday > Tuesday > Sunday > Monday. Worst: Friday and Thursday. Best hours: 12:00, 15:00, 17:00. Avoid 09:00 and 14:00. The Short simplifies this to "send Tuesday and Wednesday," which is the takeaway to keep.

## Target the person who handles the function

Directors (2,944) and Managers (1,567) received 3x more outreach than CEOs and founders combined, and reply rates beat CEO-first at firms over 50 people. For Champion brands chasing operators, this reinforces: email the head of the function, not the corner office.

## What to change in copy

1. Cut every first touch to under 100 words. If it does not fit, the pitch is not sharp enough yet.
2. Keep subjects under 30 characters. A/B a one-word subject against a subject with a number in it.
3. Send cold touches Tuesday or Wednesday, midday to early afternoon in the prospect's timezone.
4. Cap cold-sequence emails at three (first touch plus two follow-ups). Any further touch switches channel: LinkedIn, phone, WhatsApp. Never a fourth email.
