# Service Catalogue & Signal → Service Mapping

Use this file during Phase 2, Step 4 to select the 2–3 most relevant pitch options.

---

## Service Definitions

### SalesTech — Data-Driven Lead Generation
**Core offer:** Verified B2B contact data, intent-enriched prospect lists, account-level behavioural signals.
**What it solves:** SDR teams burning time on bad data, high bounce rates, low reply rates, outbound teams scaling into new geos/verticals.
**Best metric to cite:** Reduced bounce rate to <1%, 3–5x reply rate lift, 45% faster pipeline build.

---

### MarTech — Data-Driven Marketing Intelligence
**Core offer:** Audience segmentation data, email campaign lists, ABM audience builds, programmatic targeting segments.
**What it solves:** Marketing teams targeting too broadly, high cost-per-MQL, low campaign relevance, poor database hygiene for nurture flows.
**Best metric to cite:** 200%+ campaign ROI improvement, 30% reduction in CAC, 5x lead acquisition lift.

---

### RecruitTech — Recruitment Data & Intelligence
**Core offer:** Talent intelligence databases, passive candidate contact data, skills-based sourcing lists.
**What it solves:** Talent acquisition teams struggling with sourcing speed in niche skill sets, scaling into new markets, or needing verified direct contact data for passive candidates.
**Best metric to cite:** 40% faster time-to-shortlist, access to 450M+ global executive profiles.

---

### GrowthTech — Bespoke CXO-Level Solutions
**Core offer:** Strategic data consulting, custom intent cooperative builds, compliance-ready global data infrastructure.
**What it solves:** CXOs and RevOps leaders needing a bespoke data strategy — not off-the-shelf lists. Compliance requirements (GDPR, DPDP, CCPA). Custom ICP modelling, territory planning.
**Best metric to cite:** 35% shorter sales cycles, $2.8M+ pipeline generated in case studies, DPDP/GDPR-compliant infrastructure.

## Framing Rules by Service

**SalesTech pitch angle:** Always lead with data quality and deliverability outcomes, not data volume.
Bad: "We have 450M contacts."
Good: "We helped a similar team cut their bounce rate from 6% to 0.8% in two weeks."

**MarTech pitch angle:** Lead with audience precision and campaign efficiency, not list size.
Bad: "We can send you a list of CMOs."
Good: "We built an intent-verified segment that cut their CPL by 30% in one quarter."

**RecruitTech pitch angle:** Lead with speed and access to passive, hard-to-reach talent.
Bad: "We have a database of candidates."
Good: "We helped their TA team source 3 niche engineering roles in 10 days that had been open for 4 months."

**GrowthTech pitch angle:** Always position as a strategic partner, not a vendor.
Bad: "We offer custom data solutions."
Good: "We work with their RevOps team to build a compliant, territory-specific data infrastructure — not a one-size list."
