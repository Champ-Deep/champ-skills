# Outreach Messages

Three first-touch messages ready to fill in: an email under 100 words, a connection note under 300 characters, and a LinkedIn DM.

How to use: replace every {{placeholder}} with your own facts, then ask Claude to rewrite it in your voice before sending.
How to use: check the counts yourself, the email under 100 words and the connection note under 300 characters.
How to use: never send a message with a {{placeholder}} still in it, or a claim you cannot prove with something you hold.

## Email: first touch, under 100 words
Subject: {{the trigger in their words, not a pitch}}
Hi {{first name}},
I saw {{specific signal}} on {{date}}: {{one line on what it likely means for their team}}.
We help {{type of team like theirs}} {{outcome in plain words}} without {{the pain they likely have}}.
Worth 15 minutes on {{day or week}} to compare notes on {{process they run}}?
{{your name}}
{{your title}}, {{company}}
Count check: keep this under 100 words. Cut the third sentence before you cut the question.

## LinkedIn connection note, under 300 characters
{{first name}}, I saw {{signal}} at {{company}} and work on {{topic}} with {{type of team}}. Sending a note rather than a pitch. Open to connecting?
Count check: this must stay under 300 characters including the name.

## LinkedIn DM, after they accept
Thanks for connecting, {{first name}}.
{{One line on the signal you saw, with its date.}}
{{One line on the outcome you deliver. Delete this line if you have no proof point to cite.}}
Is {{process they run}} something you are reviewing this quarter, or is it settled for now?
{{your name}}

## Placeholder meanings
{{signal}}: the real event with its date, a funding round, a hire, a post, a job change.
{{process they run}}: the workflow your product touches, named the way they would name it.
{{pain they likely have}}: one concrete cost of the current way, never a list.
{{proof point}}: a customer or number you are allowed to name. No proof point means delete the line.

## Before you send
- Every {{placeholder}} replaced, none left in the text.
- Email under 100 words, connection note under 300 characters.
- The signal is named with its date, not a vague opener.
- One question only, easy to answer yes or no.
- No claim you cannot back with something you actually hold.
- You would read this if it landed on you.
