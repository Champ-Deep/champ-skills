# Call Notes

Fill-in template for call notes you paste into your CRM after any discovery or demo call.

How to use: fill this in during or right after the call, while you still remember their exact words.
How to use: paste the finished note into your CRM, then pull the next step, owner and date out at the bottom.
How to use: if a field does not apply, write "not discussed" instead of leaving it blank, so gaps stay visible.

## Filling rules
- Quotes come from the call as spoken; paraphrases are labelled paraphrase.
- "Not discussed" beats a guess in every field.
- Every call leaves one next step with an owner and a date, or "date not set" flagged as a gap.
- No adjectives of enthusiasm: no great call, no strong interest, no hot lead.
- Names and titles appear only if someone actually said them on the call.
