## Sequence rules
- Day gaps are fixed: 1, 3, 7, 14, 21, 30. Do not add touches between them.
- Each touch adds something new or stops; never resend the same email twice.
- Any reply ends the sequence immediately, even a no.
- Log every touch with its actual date in your tracker before sending the next one.
- One question per touch, and never a question you could have looked up.
- If you cannot fill {{placeholder}} with a real fact, the touch is not ready to send.
