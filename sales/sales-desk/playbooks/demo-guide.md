# Demo Guide

Structures a 30-minute demo: open, confirm pain, show three moments, handle questions, close with a next step.

## Give Claude
- Attendees and their titles, plus what they said in discovery (paste it)
- Your product's main capabilities, listed plainly
- Optional: their current tool or workflow
- Optional: the deal stage and what you want agreed after the demo
- Optional: the length of the slot, if it is not 30 minutes

## Claude does
1. Allocate the minutes: open 3, confirm pain 4, show 15, handle 5, close 3, with a stated hard stop.
2. Write the open: two sentences restating their stated pain in their own words, then a one-line agenda.
3. Pick exactly three moments to show, each mapped to a pain they named, with the single screen or outcome that proves it.
4. For each moment, write what to show, what to say in one line, and what to deliberately skip.
5. List the likely questions for this audience with a one-line answer each, plus two questions to ask them instead of answering.
6. Write the close: the specific next step, who does it, by when, asked out loud.
7. Add the fallback: if they will not commit, agree a dated follow-up with a named attendee.
8. Mark the moment to slow down: the screen where buyers usually stall or ask to try it themselves.

## Output
- Timed agenda: `minute range | segment | what happens | your line`.
- Three moments: `pain | what to show | what to say | what to skip`.
- Question handling: question, one-line answer, or the counter-question instead.
- The closing ask verbatim, and the fallback ask verbatim.
- One line on what to cut if the demo runs long.

## Rules
- Three moments, never a feature tour. A fourth means cutting the weakest.
- Every moment traces to a pain in the pasted discovery notes.
- Never promise dates, pricing, integrations or roadmaps that were not given.
- The close names a specific next step, never "let's keep talking".
- The agenda stays inside the slot including questions; if it does not, cut scope, not time.
- Do not demo features for roles who are not on the call.
