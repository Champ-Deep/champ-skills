# REACH: Research (Stage R)

Turn a name and a URL into a brief you can act on in minutes. Know more about the account than they expect. Tiered: a 5-minute scan for volume, a 30-minute deep dive for accounts that matter.

## Tailor this first (edit once, then save your copy)

Replace the bracketed values with your reality. The whole skill uses them.

```
WHAT WE SELL: [one clear line, e.g. "B2B contact data and AI SDR tooling"]
OUR ICP: [who we sell to, e.g. "RevOps and demand-gen leaders at 200 to 5,000 person B2B firms"]
TOP COMPETITORS: [list 2 to 4]
DATA SOURCES I HAVE: [LinkedIn, Apollo, Ahrefs, company site, none]
```

## When to use

A rep is about to reach out to a prospect or wants to understand an account. Trigger before Engage. If the rep only gives a name, ask for the company and a URL.

## The workflow

1. Collect: prospect name and title, company, a URL (site or LinkedIn), and the tier (scan or deep dive). Default to scan.
2. Produce the brief in exactly this format, no fluff:
   - SNAPSHOT: size, industry, what they actually do, recent funding or news (last 6 months).
   - SIGNALS: hiring trends, tech stack hints, leadership changes, regulatory or market shifts.
   - THE WEDGE: the single most likely problem WHAT WE SELL solves for them, tied to a public signal.
   - WHY NOW: why an outreach today is relevant, not generic.
   - THE PERSON: what this buyer likely cares about given role and seniority.
   - OPENING HOOK: one sentence that proves real homework.
3. If deep dive, also add: STAKEHOLDER MAP (5 to 7 people, who blocks, who champions), PAIN-POINT MAP (3 signals to 3 problems with cost of inaction), COMPETITIVE POSITION (what they use today, the gap, switching cost), TRIGGER EVENTS to monitor, the NARRATIVE (problem, cost of inaction, our approach, proof, next step), and a 3-touch ACCOUNT PLAN.
4. Never invent facts or numbers. If unsure, say so and mark it for the rep to verify.

## Output

A clean, skimmable brief the rep can read in under 5 minutes (scan) or use as an account plan (deep dive). End with the single most important next action.

## Power-ups (optional, not required)

- Skills: `account-research`, `meeting-prep`, `firecrawl` for live web research.
- Connectors: Proxycurl or Apollo for enrichment, Ahrefs for competitor and traffic signals.
- Champ stack: ChampPulse for intent, ChampScan for web enrichment.

## Hand off to

`reach-engage` to turn this brief into the wedge email.
