# REACH: Hold (Stage H)

This is the hour you win back. Account depth. Become the person an account loops in before a decision is made, not after. Deals become a byproduct of the relationship.

## Tailor this first (edit once, then save your copy)

```
WHAT WE SELL: [one line]
HOW I TIER ACCOUNTS: [e.g. "Anchor, Active, Passive, Dormant by value and warmth"]
MY DEFAULT NEXT STEP: [e.g. "a specific 3-week follow-up with something useful first"]
```

## When to use

The rep has a live or existing account and wants to deepen it or prepare for a conversation. Two modes: a no-agenda Pulse, and Call Prep.

## The workflow

### Mode 1: Account-Depth Pulse
Given the account and the last conversation, return:
1. WHAT MAY HAVE CHANGED since you last spoke (news, hiring, market).
2. THE OPENING QUESTION, a "what's changed since we last spoke" opener tuned to them.
3. ONE USEFUL THING TO BRING (a data point or insight relevant whether they buy more or not).
4. EXPANSION SIGNALS to listen for (phrases meaning budget, a new problem, or a referral).
5. THE CLEAR NEXT (a specific next touchpoint, never "let me know if you need anything").

### Mode 2: Call Prep
Given the brief and the call goal, return:
1. THREE SMART QUESTIONS that surface the real problem (problem and implication style).
2. THE LIKELY 3 OBJECTIONS and a one-line response to each.
3. THE PROOF to have ready (the single most relevant case or number).
4. THE TRAP TO AVOID (the one thing that would make this buyer disengage).
5. THE CLOSE (the low-friction next step to ask for, and a fallback).

Keep everything relationship-first. The goal is trust, not a pitch.

## Output

A short prep sheet the rep can scan before the call or check-in.
