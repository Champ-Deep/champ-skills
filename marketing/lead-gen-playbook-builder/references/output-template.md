# Output Template: the finished playbook structure

The finished playbook is one self-contained document a rep can execute from with zero prior context. Use this exact section order. Open with the TL;DR so a rep who reads only the first screen still knows the win condition and the first move.

```markdown
# {{Campaign}} Lead-Gen Playbook

> One-line positioning. Owner, team, window, win condition by date.

## 0. TL;DR (read this first)
- The win condition (the number, by the date).
- The single most important strategic choice (e.g., lead with problem not asset).
- The first physical action a rep takes Monday morning.

## 1. The offer and the proof
- What we sell / invite to, the buyer outcome, the confirmed proof (flag confirmed vs aspirational), the pricing posture.

## 2. Personas (who we chase)
For each of 3-6 personas: data signature, real example accounts, what keeps them up at night, why we chase them, priority, where to pull them from in the data.

## 3. The channel waterfall (cadence)
Per-tier touch tables: day, channel, round, intent, what goes out. Daily quotas per rep.

## 4. Exact pitches (per persona x channel x round)
The actual copy with merge fields. Email, LinkedIn note + DM, WhatsApp, phone + voicemail, social DM, newsletter. Personalize line one, send the rest.

## 5. Decision flowcharts (reply handling)
Mermaid flowchart per channel + the per-signal action/SLA/script tables. Objection sub-flow.

## 6. The operating system
- Real-time tracker schema (stages, owner, last touch, the live count).
- Reply-handling SLAs.
- Pricing discipline (when/what a rep may quote, what needs sign-off).
- Hard don'ts.
- Gates with dates (where a cascade could happen; what triggers a slip or escalation).

## 7. Weekly review cadence
Metrics, targets by week, iteration rules (what to change if a number is below target).

## 8. Files / related
Links to the contact data, samples, one-pagers, and related vault notes.
```

## Quality bar before you ship
- A rep with zero context can execute from this document alone.
- Every cadence touch has actual copy, not a description of copy.
- Every reply signal has a next action, an SLA, and a script.
- Anchor/proof discipline, pricing discipline, and compliance are explicit.
- The win condition is a number with a date, and there is at least one dated gate.
- Personas map to real rows the rep can pull today.
