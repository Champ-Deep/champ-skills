## LinkedIn post copy

Most SDR teams asking for more leads already have thousands of accounts. What they lack is a rule for which forty to call this week.

It is 8:40 on a Tuesday. The rep opens a saved view: 412 accounts, all in the ICP, sorted by company name. Nothing in it says who hired a VP of Sales three weeks ago or who had four people reading about the category last Thursday. So the first hour goes to a company that will not buy for eighteen months.

That is a sequencing failure, and buying more contacts does not fix it.

We wrote down the operating rules we use to turn a static list into a ranked one: four signal families, a six-dimension scorecard that takes ninety seconds, tiers with gates, a five-minute research block that ends in one written hypothesis, and a message framework that never mentions the data.

Ten slides, four minutes. Everything runs in a spreadsheet before it is worth automating.
