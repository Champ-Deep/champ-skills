# RACE funnel diagnosis

Score the client's four funnel stages and work only on the worst one. Use when the ask is "grow this business", "find the bottleneck", or a growth plan drawn from a course or playbook rather than from an audit of one site.

## The four stages

| Stage | Question | Typical evidence source |
|---|---|---|
| Reach | Are we being seen? | organic keyword positions, ad spend by geo, site bounce rate |
| Acquire | Does interest become a lead someone owns? | positive replies vs CRM leads, form fills vs assigned leads, time to first touch, inbox placement |
| Convert | Does the lead become revenue? | close rate, win rate, stage conversion |
| Expand | Does the customer buy again? | repeat and renewal rates, dormant account revenue, upsell share |

## Procedure

1. Ask for one number per stage before forming a view. Stage ratings that skip this step are guesses.
2. Where a number does not exist, that absence is itself a finding. "Not measured" ranks above a soft number, because an unmeasured stage cannot be shown to be the bottleneck or to have improved.
3. Rank stages by worst rate relative to target, not by worst absolute number: a stage at target cannot be the bottleneck even if it looks small.
4. Pick one stage. Do not plan work on the others until the chosen one has a baseline and a fix shipped.
5. Factor that one goal into concrete base actions, each mapped to an existing task/card or proposed as a new one with owner and acceptance test.
6. Baseline the fix before touching it, re-measure after a fixed interval, then and only then re-rank the stages.

## Ordering rules of thumb

- Leaks come before volume. If leads arrive faster than they are handled, Reach is not the bottleneck: more top-of-funnel traffic just feeds the leak.
- Expand follows Acquire when a proven reactivation or upsell motion already exists, because scaling a working motion beats inventing one.
- New product or feature builds are not fixes for a funnel bottleneck until evidence shows the missing capability is the bottleneck. This is the single most common way a growth plan drifts into the wrong lane.

## Where this sits against a site audit

A site audit ranks defects on one surface. This ranks the whole customer funnel across systems (site, CRM, outbound tooling, billing). They compose: run the funnel diagnosis first to pick the stage, then a site audit inside that stage's surface if the fix touches the site.

## Reporting

Lead with the bottleneck and the evidence for it, then the factoring table, then the guardrails. Label every figure with its source and date, and mark figures taken from meeting notes or other people's recollection as reported, not checked. Name the irreversible actions and who approves them; never stage or take them yourself.
