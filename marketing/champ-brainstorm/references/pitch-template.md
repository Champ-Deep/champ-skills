# Pitch Deck / Proposal Outline Template — CHAMP Framework

Use this template when generating a sales pitch or customer proposal outline after a
Sales Pitch-mode brainstorming session. This provides a slide-by-slide structure that
can be used to build a presentation deck or a written proposal document.

---

```markdown
# [Proposal/Pitch Title]
## Prepared for [Client/Prospect Name]

**Prepared by:** [Author / Company]
**Date:** [Date]

---

### Slide 1: The Hook
[Open with the prospect's biggest pain point, stated dramatically.
Use their language. Make them feel seen.]

### Slide 2: The Cost of Inaction
[Quantify what the problem is costing them — revenue lost, time wasted,
opportunity cost, competitive risk. Use data if available.]

### Slide 3: The Opportunity
[Reframe the problem as an opportunity. What's possible if this is solved?
Paint the aspirational picture.]

### Slide 4: Our Approach
[How we solve this. Focus on methodology, not features. Show that you
understand their world and have a proven playbook.]

### Slide 5: The Solution in Action
[Concrete example of what the engagement looks like. Timeline, deliverables,
touchpoints. Make it tangible.]

### Slide 6: Personalization / Why This Is Built for You
[Show that this isn't a generic pitch. Reference specific details about their
business, industry, or challenges. The more specific, the more powerful.]

### Slide 7: Proof Points
[Case studies, testimonials, metrics from past work. Choose examples that
mirror the prospect's situation as closely as possible.]

| Client | Challenge | Result |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| [Client A] | [Similar challenge] | [Quantified outcome] |
| [Client B] | [Similar challenge] | [Quantified outcome] |

### Slide 8: Investment & ROI
[Pricing/engagement structure. Frame as investment, not cost.
Include projected ROI where possible.]

### Slide 9: The Ask
[Clear, specific next step. Not "let us know" — instead,
"Here's what we propose as next steps" with a timeline.]

### Slide 10: Appendix / Deep Dive (Optional)
[Additional detail for prospects who want to dig deeper —
team bios, methodology details, technical specs, etc.]

---

## Proposal Document Variant

If the user prefers a written proposal over a slide deck, expand each slide
into a section with 2-4 paragraphs. Maintain the same flow and narrative arc.
Add an executive summary at the top (1 paragraph, captures slides 1-3 in essence)
and a terms/next-steps section at the bottom.

---

*Generated via CHAMP Framework — Champions Accelerator*
```
