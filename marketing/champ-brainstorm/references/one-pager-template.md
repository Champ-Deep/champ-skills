# CHAMP One-Pager Template

Use this template when generating the one-pager summary after a brainstorming session.
Replace all bracketed content with session-specific insights. Keep it dense and scannable —
this should fit on a single page when printed.

---

```markdown
# [Project/Campaign/Product Name] — CHAMP One-Pager

**Mode:** [Product / Campaign / Sales Pitch]
**Date:** [Session Date]
**Owner:** [User Name / Team]

---

## C — Customer [or Challenges]

**Primary Persona:** [Vivid 1-2 sentence description of the target customer]

**Core Pain Point:** [The #1 problem, stated in the customer's own language]

**Key Segments:** [2-3 micro-segments identified, with differentiating characteristics]

---

## H — Hypothesis

> "We believe [customer segment] will [desired behavior/response] because [unique insight]."

**Riskiest Assumption:** [The one thing that, if wrong, breaks everything]

**Validation Approach:** [How to test cheaply and quickly]

---

## A — Approach [or Actions]

| Phase | What | Timeline | Cost Estimate |
|-------|------|----------|---------------|
| 1 | [MVP / Initial Launch / First Meeting] | [Timeframe] | [Budget] |
| 2 | [Iteration / Optimization / Follow-up] | [Timeframe] | [Budget] |
| 3 | [Scale / Expansion / Close] | [Timeframe] | [Budget] |

**Personalization Strategy:** [How this adapts across segments — the zip-code-level detail]

---

## M — Market [or Metrics]

**Market Size:** [TAM/SAM/SOM or Campaign Reach]

**Positioning:** For [target], [product/campaign] is the [category] that [benefit] unlike [competitor].

**Key Metrics:**
| Metric | Target | Measurement Method |
|--------|--------|--------------------|
| [North Star / Primary KPI] | [Target] | [How measured] |
| [Leading Indicator] | [Target] | [How measured] |
| [Guardrail Metric] | [Threshold] | [How measured] |

---

## P — Pivot [or Priorities]

**Pivot Triggers:** [Specific signals that indicate a need to change course]

**Plan B:** [Primary alternative if the hypothesis fails]

**Kill Criteria:** [When to walk away]

**Milestone Roadmap:**
MVP → [Gate 1 criteria] → Milestone 1 → [Gate 2 criteria] → Milestone 2

---

*Generated via CHAMP Framework Brainstorming — Champions Accelerator*
```
