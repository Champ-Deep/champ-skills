# Opportunity Scoring Framework

Use this rubric during Mode A (Pipeline Scan) to rank clients by case study potential.
Score each client on the six dimensions below. Sum the scores. Use the tier thresholds
to assign a recommended action.

---

## Scoring Dimensions (0-3 each, max 18)

### 1. Outcome Strength (0-3)
Does this client have documented, measurable results?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 3 | Hard metrics on record: leads generated, revenue impact, event attendance, conversion rate, response rate |
| 2 | Partial metrics: one number confirmed, others self-reported or estimated |
| 1 | Qualitative outcome only: "they were happy", "it went well", client approval of deliverables |
| 0 | No outcome data available |

### 2. Niche Value (0-3)
How rare is this sector in your current case study library?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 3 | No existing case study in this industry vertical at all |
| 2 | One case study exists but it is dated (>18 months) or weak |
| 1 | Good coverage exists but this client represents a stronger example |
| 0 | Sector is well-covered with multiple strong, current case studies |

### 3. Deal Size Signal (0-3)
Does the engagement size signal that similar deals are worth chasing?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 3 | High-ticket engagement: enterprise-level contract, multi-service scope, or premium segment (CXO data, event-grade outreach) |
| 2 | Mid-market: meaningful deal but not in the top tier |
| 1 | SMB or entry-level engagement |
| 0 | Not enough deal size information to assess |

### 4. Verifiability (0-3)
Can the claims be confirmed before publishing?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 3 | Data on file: delivery records, campaign reports, or CS documentation available |
| 2 | Client verbal confirmation available; follow-up documentation possible |
| 1 | Data is self-reported by client; some verification risk |
| 0 | No clear verification path; high dispute risk |

### 5. Client Approachability (0-3)
Is the client likely to allow naming and quoting?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 3 | Strong relationship; client has already spoken positively on record |
| 2 | Good relationship; approval likely with a simple ask |
| 1 | Neutral or uncertain; may require legal review or prefer anonymization |
| 0 | Client has indicated discomfort with public mentions, or relationship is strained |

### 6. Story Richness (0-3)
Is there a compelling before/after narrative?

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 3 | Clear "before" pain point documented, transformation evident, contrast is sharp |
| 2 | Before state inferable; after state documented |
| 1 | Generic engagement with no clear transformation story |
| 0 | Transactional relationship with no narrative arc |

---

## Priority Tiers

| Total Score | Priority | Recommended Action |
|-------------|----------|--------------------|
| 15-18 | **Build Now** | Begin immediately. This is your strongest asset in this vertical. |
| 11-14 | **Build Soon** | High value. Queue within the next 30 days. |
| 7-10 | **Track** | Promising but incomplete. Set a follow-up date to reassess. |
| 4-6 | **Develop** | Relationship or data needs more time. Come back in 60-90 days. |
| 0-3 | **Skip** | Not worth pursuing now. Revisit only if situation changes significantly. |

---

## Niche + High-Ticket Prioritization Override

If a client scores 3 on both Niche Value AND Deal Size (total at least 6 from those two
dimensions alone), escalate their priority by one tier regardless of total score.

The rationale: one well-placed case study in an untouched vertical with enterprise deal
signals can unlock an entire segment. This is worth extra investment even when other
dimensions are weaker.

---

## Anonymization as a Fallback

When a client scores 3 on Outcome Strength and Niche Value but 0-1 on Approachability,
consider an anonymized case study. A well-written anonymized study — "A leading technology
infrastructure company in Bangalore" — is still more convincing than no proof at all.
Anonymized studies should:
- Keep all hard metrics intact
- Remove all identifying details (company name, executive names, locations if specific)
- Add a brief note: "Client details anonymized at client request"

Demotion rule: an anonymized case study scores 1 point lower on effectiveness compared to a
named one. Factor this into your priority recommendation.
