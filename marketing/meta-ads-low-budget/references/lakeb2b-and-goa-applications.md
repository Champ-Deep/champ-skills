## B. Champions Yacht Club Goa

### Where it stands (from the vault)

- The 16 Jun 2026 strategy memo (`Efforts/Active/Champions Yacht Club Onboarding/Champions-Yachts-Goa-Strategy-Memo.html`) calls paid ads to acquire each tourist "the wrong game": retail rides sell at 1,500 to 3,000 a head, charters and curated events at 50,000 to several lakh per booking. Target: three resort or villa partnerships and two corporate accounts per quarter. Retail and OTAs stay as overflow.
- 6 Oct 2026: Champions Club positioned as one luxury concierge umbrella, "crafting memorable experiences on land and water". A content creator was to be onboarded this quarter for authentic Goa footage; Goa reputation concerns flagged, CSR and beach clean-up proposed.
- Fleet claim conflict: the June memo says "we do not own yachts in Goa"; the 21 Oct note says a network of 20 or more yachts, 4 owned. Do not write "our fleet" in any ad until the owner confirms.
- Partner Boat Terms and Conditions are a draft pending legal review in India and the UAE; booking confirmation runs on WhatsApp via DoubleTick.

### Fit verdict

Good fit for a paid-social test, but only on the high-ticket side. Do not spend ad money on the 1,500 rupee ride. The video's high-value offer rule matches the memo's charter and event game.

### Offer (draft for owner approval)

A planned private charter or group day on the water: corporate offsite, celebration, wedding group. The hero mechanism is that one planner assembles and runs the whole day with vetted partner boats, instead of the beach-agent shuffle. CONFIRM: prices or price bands, group sizes, season dates, what "vetted" means in practice, and who signs it off. Real urgency: dated capacity for the season, only when true.

### The report variant

"Goa Group Day on the Water: Planning Guide". Content: how to choose a boat and operator, safety questions to ask, what a day costs by group size (only with confirmed bands), timing, rain and sea-condition backup, what to ask for in writing. Opt-in captures name, phone or WhatsApp number, group type and size, month. Rules: safety content must match the operator terms and not outrun the legal review.

### Angle research

TripAdvisor and Google reviews of Goa boat experiences, Reddit threads about Goa trips and boats, corporate offsite planner conversations. Look for: scams and surprise charges, crowding, safety worry, weather cancellations, group logistics. Quote with links.

### Two draft angles (hypotheses)

| Angle | The belief | Headline direction | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| "No surprises, one planner" | Beach boat deals are chaotic and overcharge | One person plans the whole group day | Raw native: a real group on a real deck |
| "The offsite they will talk about" | Offsites are forgettable | Move the offsite onto the water | Breaking-news look: a bold line over one striking photo |

Real photos only (Deep's standing taste; the content creator's footage is the source). No stock yachts that are not ours or a partner's.

### Test design (PROPOSED)

Broad audience in the cities where offsite and celebration buyers live (CONFIRM list), ad-set budgets equal, one ad set per ad, three or four ads at most across the two angles. Leads go to WhatsApp and the CRM with source, angle and look. A Goa-side caller phones in 60 seconds, asks the five questions, and logs the exact phrases. Success is a qualified enquiry and a booked proposal, not clicks. Budget, season window, stop rule: CONFIRM with Deep.

### Risks

Reputation concerns in Goa; unconfirmed fleet ownership; terms pending legal review; marine safety claims; partner capacity; consent for calling and WhatsApp (DPDP and TRAI; not checked). Weather and sea-condition cancellations must be in the page.
