# Paid social experiment controls

Source: https://youtu.be/u57s5wmdl5s, supplied by Deep on 11 Nov 2026 with his six-tactic summary. The transcript was checked on 11 Nov 2026 (auto captions); the timestamped notes are in `video-evidence.md`. Controls below are our extensions, not claims that the creator prescribed them. Our CRM-qualified outcomes and approved brand plans outrank borrowed benchmarks.

## Research and testing

Collect public Reddit threads, Trustpilot and other relevant competitor reviews: permalink, visible date, short verbatim excerpt, context, alleged problem and source type. Deduplicate cross-posts and report the actual retrieved count. Search failure is not evidence a complaint does not exist. Complaints are self-selected allegations, not proven facts or market prevalence. Separate quotes from inferred angles. Do not repurpose reviewers' identities for ad audiences. Use B2B evidence for LakeB2B/SPAN, separate tourism/planner evidence for Goa.

Test two belief/pain angles with offer and static format held fixed. If affordable, a later four-cell angle/format test must report both dimensions. Separate ad sets can overlap and do not prove randomization; label comparisons directional unless a real split test was run. Broad means within approved geography, language, age and eligibility, not all markets or verified job-role selection on Meta. Check current platform capabilities and policy.

Write the cap, primary qualified outcome, review window and pause/escalation rules before launch. Choose evidence thresholds from expected qualified-lead cost and sales-cycle lag, not a universal 30 to 50 leads or seven days. A small budget can produce inconclusive results; never exceed it to hit a sample target. Report spend, valid/reachable leads, qualified leads, opportunities/bookings, dates and denominators. CBO and 80/20 are optional later tests after real evidence and Deep approval, never automatic budget changes. AI drafts analysis only.

Compute every readout date in code from the launch or ruling date and compare it to the season dates it is argued against (a 30 day read from 18 Nov lands 18 Dec, before Christmas week, not after). Check any "no excerpt is dated X" line against each excerpt's own date field, and any "owner named in <card>" line against that card's body and comments, before it goes into a strategy file or a decision comment.

## Report-led offer and 500 validations

Deep proposed 500 free validations of a buyer-supplied file. This is a proposed cap, not a live entitlement. Validation, contact enrichment and messaging simulation are different deliverables. Confirm eligible rows, supported fields, checks performed, catch-all/unknown handling, deduplication, exclusions, delivery owner, turnaround and method before publishing. A hero mechanism must be real; urgency, safety and fleet claims require evidence.

A file picker does not prove a secure service exists. Backend uploads require size/type controls, access control, relevant malware/formula handling, retention/deletion rules, a named processor and legal/consent review. Do not upload a prospect's contact file into an LLM or third-party tool just to demonstrate the offer. Mockups use non-personal data labelled illustrative.

A messaging report shows sourced current copy, intended audience, explicitly simulated reactions, friction, suggested changes and a live test proposal. It never promises predicted conversion, ROAS or revenue. A generic guide is not a bespoke simulation unless the actual run exists.

## Follow-up and attribution

A 60-second contact target applies only to consented enquiries in staffed local calling hours. Distinguish form submit, attempt, connected conversation and qualification; log actual timestamps/latency. Define after-hours acknowledgement, opt-out and deduplication. A download is not blanket phone/WhatsApp consent or a 24/7 SLA.

Deep named Pallab Majinder as a possible caller. Verify identity and capacity, do not equate him with ads operator Pallab P. Do not make staffing or performance decisions from a proposed campaign. LakeB2B APAC client/vendor coordination remains Rupak's. Goa consumer enquiries need a named Goa-side owner, not automatically Deep or the B2B sales team. Deep's planning window is 15:00 to 02:00 IST; customer contact follows local rules and their time zone.

Preserve campaign/angle/creative ids and UTMs through opt-in, CRM and outcomes; deduplicate platform lead ids. Separate click-through, view-through and CRM attribution. For Ryze, distinguish recommendations, accepted recommendations, executed changes and outcomes. Trial approval is not proof the trial started or performed. Audit account exports and change history before asserting improvement.
