# Campaign Brief Template — CHAMP Framework

Use this template when the user requests a campaign brief after a Campaign-mode brainstorming
session. This should be a document that a creative team or marketing agency can pick up and
execute from. The emphasis is on bold creative direction, hyper-personalization strategy,
and measurable outcomes.
