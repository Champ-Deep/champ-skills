# [[Lake B2B|LakeB2B]]: Voice and Tone (for documents)

Confident, enterprise credible, partnership led. We speak to CXOs and revenue
leaders. We lean on depth, precision, and outcomes, and we avoid hype. The
category is "the B2B growth stack." The promise is ENABLING GROWTH.

## Personality and tone

| Attribute | Means |
|-----------|-------|
| Intelligent without jargon | Smart, clear, no buzzwords |
| Direct without blunt | Say it plainly, stay respectful |
| Ambitious without arrogant | Confident about outcomes, never boastful |
| Warm without casual | Human and partnership led, still enterprise |

## Positioning hierarchy

Anchor every major document to this ladder. The category sits above the promise.

| Layer | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Category | The B2B growth stack |
| Promise | ENABLING GROWTH |
| Proof | Four integrated verticals: SalesTech, MarTech, RecruitTech, GrowthTech |
| Foundation | Deep data reservoirs, the depth of the lake |
| Outcome | Pipeline, campaigns, talent, and CXO level strategy |

Why "stack" matters: it signals modularity, integration, and full lifecycle
coverage. It separates [[Lake B2B|LakeB2B]] from single point data vendors and positions it as
infrastructure for enterprise growth.

## Lexicon

Use: growth stack, stack, integrated, platform, scale, depth, precision,
intelligence, pipeline, outcomes, partnership, enterprise, reservoir, revenue
motion, full lifecycle.

Avoid: disrupt, revolutionary, game changer, synergy, ideate, circle back, best
in class (when unsupported), point solution, vendor.

Signatures:
- Category phrase: "the B2B growth stack" (lowercase "the" and "stack", unless
  rendered as a badge or all-caps lockup).
- Promise phrase: ENABLING GROWTH (always uppercase as the brand promise).

## Mechanics

- No em-dashes anywhere. Use periods, commas, colons, or restructure.
- Naming: small caps means no space (SalesTech). All caps means a single space
  (SALES TECH). Write "[[Lake B2B|LakeB2B]]" with no space, except in prose where "[[Lake B2B]]"
  is allowed, or in all-caps lockups (LAKE B2B).
- Numbers: numerals for data and metrics. Be precise, cite the unit.
- Sentences: clear and measured. Confidence comes from precision, not volume.
