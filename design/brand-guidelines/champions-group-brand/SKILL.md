# Champions Group Brand Guidelines

Brand guidelines for Champions Group, enabling creation of premium on-brand content and materials with the signature **orange + white** identity.

> **UPDATED 2026-04-10:** Primary palette migrated from charcoal + gold to orange + white to match the current Champions Group logo. All new materials must use orange + white. Re-skin older materials on touch.

## Brand Overview

**Company:** Champions Group
**Tagline:** Empowering Innovation. Building Resilience.
**Identity:** Parent holding company overseeing 12 ventures across data intelligence, technology, wellness, and startup acceleration
**HQ:** Bangalore, India (with US presence via subsidiary Ampliz in Emeryville, CA)
**Founder & CEO:** Sreedeep Surapaneni (Group CMO across all 12 companies)

Champions Group is the umbrella entity that houses the Champions Ecosystem (Champions Accelerator, Champions Infometrics, Champions Club, Recruit Champ), the .fit Ecosystem (Champ.fit, Health.fit, Food.fit), Data & Services (Lake B2B, SPAN Global Services, IP Momentum, Cirralogix), and Technology (InfraTech).

### Typography

- **Primary:** Inter (web), Calibri (documents). Both modern, geometric, high legibility.
- **Fallback:** -apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, Segoe UI, Arial, sans-serif
- **Headlines:** Inter/Calibri Bold, 24-48pt, Champions Orange #F26722 or Ink #1A1A1A
- **Subheadings:** Inter/Calibri SemiBold, 14-18pt, Ink #1A1A1A
- **Body:** Inter/Calibri Regular, 14-16px (web), 10.5-11pt (docs), Ink #1A1A1A
- **Captions/Meta:** Inter/Calibri Regular, 11-12px, Warm Gray #6B6B6B
- **CTAs/Buttons:** Inter/Calibri SemiBold, sentence case (not ALL CAPS), letter-spacing 0.01em

## Logo & Identity Rules

- Champions Group wordmark uses Inter/Calibri Bold with the crown/shield icon, rendered in Champions Orange #F26722
- Minimum digital width: 160px. Minimum print width: 30mm.
- Clear space: Height of the "C" on all sides.
- Approved backgrounds: White (primary), Off White, Peach, Orange (wordmark inverted to white).
- Orange elements: Use #F26722 on light backgrounds. Use #FFFFFF wordmark on #F26722 backgrounds.

**Never:** Stretch, rotate, recolor outside palette, add drop shadows, place on busy/patterned backgrounds, use gradient fills on the wordmark itself, combine with subsidiary logos in the same lockup.

## Document Design Patterns

### Cover Pages
- White background with full-width orange hero band, OR full orange with white text
- Title in Orange #F26722 on white, or White on orange, 28-36pt Bold
- Orange divider line (2pt, #F26722) separating title from metadata
- Generous whitespace

### Section Headings
- H1: 16pt Bold, Ink #1A1A1A, with orange underline accent (2pt, #F26722)
- H2: 13pt Bold, Ink Soft #2E2E2E
- Section numbers in Orange #F26722

### Tables
- Header row: Champions Orange #F26722 background, White text, Bold
- Alternating rows: White / Soft Peach #FFF4EC
- Borders: Border Gray #E5E7EB (1pt)
- Highlight rows: Warm Tint #FFE8D6

### Buttons & CTAs (web)
- **Primary:** #F26722 background, white text, 12-16px padding, 8-10px radius, hover #D94F0A
- **Secondary:** White background, #F26722 border (1.5px) and text, hover fills Soft Peach #FFF4EC
- **Tertiary:** Ghost button, Ink text, hover adds Soft Peach background


### Critical docx-js Rules (Champions Group Context)
1. **ShadingType.CLEAR**, never SOLID
2. **WidthType.DXA**, never PERCENTAGE for table widths
3. **Tables need dual widths**
4. **Never use `\n`** in TextRun
5. **Never use unicode bullets**, use numbering config with LevelFormat.BULLET
6. **PageBreak** must be inside a Paragraph child
7. **ImageRun** requires explicit `type` property
8. **Font: "Calibri"** on every TextRun
9. **Validation:** Always run `python validate.py <file>.docx` after generation

### Contrast Rules (A7 in CLAUDE.md, strict enforcement)
- Orange #F26722 background: use White #FFFFFF text. Never Ink.
- White background: use Ink #1A1A1A for body, Orange #F26722 for accents and headlines.
- Soft Peach #FFF4EC background: use Ink #1A1A1A text. Orange OK for accent only.
- Never: light gray text on white, orange text on peach, orange text on warm tint.
