## Logo

- Stretched hexagon badge in AB7 Blue, thin white inner keyline, heavy white "AB7" wordmark. One locked unit: never redraw, recolour, stretch, rotate, outline, shadow or apply gradients to it.
- Backgrounds: white, Mist #F8FAFC, or Midnight #050B17. Never directly on AB7 Blue or the hero gradient (blue on blue loses the edge); put it in a white container if needed.
- Clear space: at least one quarter of the badge width on all sides.
- Minimum size: 32 px on screen, 12 mm in print. Placement: top left on covers and slide masters; 18 to 24 px in running headers.
- The only available file is a low-res raster. Do not upscale past about 100 px. Flag this to the user on any print or large-format job.

### Gradients

- **Signature (text fill):** 90deg, #0074D9 0%, #14D8A6 55%, #00D370 100%. Used on the second line of a headline. Max one use per page or slide, 3 to 5 words.
- **Hero (backgrounds):** 135deg, #003F7A, #005FB1 45%, #0B1324. For covers, title slides and opening web bands: white headline, Sky eyebrow, green button.

### Proportion and contrast

- Typical document: White 60%, Navy/Midnight 25%, Blue 10%, Green 5%.
- Navy on white 17.9:1, Slate on white 7.6:1, white on Blue 4.7:1 (14 px bold and up), Midnight on Green 9.9:1. White on Green and Green on white are 2.0:1: never use for text.

## Typography

One family: **Inter** (Google Fonts), fallback Arial. Headlines are heavy with tight negative tracking.

| Role | Web | Word / PDF | Slides 16:9 | Weight, tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cover title | 44 to 50 px | 34 pt | 44 pt | 800, -35 |
| Heading 1 | 28 to 34 px | 22 pt | 32 pt | 800, -25 |
| Heading 2 | 20 to 22 px | 15 pt | 22 pt | 700, -15 |
| Eyebrow | 11 px caps | 7.5 pt caps | 11 pt caps | 700, +240 |
| Body | 15 to 16 px | 10 pt on 15 pt | 16 pt | 400, 0 |
| Caption, footer | 12 px | 7.5 pt | 10 pt | 500, 0 |

Tracking is in Word/PowerPoint units (thousandths of an em). Numbers in stats use tabular figures.

## Components

- **Buttons:** 6 px radius, weight 700. Primary is Action Green with Midnight label. Secondary is ghost (white 5% fill, white 15% border) on dark, or Blue / Blue outline on light. Never two green buttons side by side.
- **Cards and panels:** 16 px radius. Dark cards one step lighter than their band (Deep Navy on Midnight), 1 px white 10% border.
- **Chips and tags:** full pill radius, Blue Tint fill with Blue text, or green tint #ECFDF5 with #047857 text.
- **Eyebrows:** short uppercase lines with middle dots, for example "Established 2015 · One partner · Seven pillars". Sky on dark, Blue on light.
- **Credentials card:** Deep Navy card, Sky eyebrow "Credentials at a glance", 2x2 grid of 800-weight stats with Muted labels.
- **Numbering:** 01 to 07 is reserved for the seven service lines. Don't use two-digit numbering for unrelated lists.

## Document layout (A4 and slides)

- **Cover:** hero gradient background, logo top left, Sky eyebrow (doc type), white 800 title with the second line in the signature gradient, one-line Slate-200 subtitle, footer rule with "Prepared for [Client]" and "[Month Year] · Confidential".
- **Interior pages:** white, 20 mm margins. Running header: logo at 6 mm, "AB7 Solutions · [Doc title]", page number, 7.5 pt Muted, Line rule below. Section opener = eyebrow, heading, one short paragraph, then evidence.
- **Tables:** Navy Ink header row with white text, Line rules, no zebra fill.
- **Accent:** at most one 3 pt gradient rule (22% width) per page.
- **Footer band:** Midnight, with ab7solutions.com, US and India phones, and "Confidential".
- **Slides:** same structure on a 16:9 master; hero gradient for title and section slides, white for content.
- **File naming:** `AB7_[Client]_[DocType]_v[n]_[YYYY-MM-DD]`, e.g. `AB7_KW-DelhiNCR_Proposal_v1_2026-09-24.pdf`.

## Voice

Operator-led, specific, low risk. Write like a delivery lead talking to a buyer.

- **Specific:** every claim carries a number, time or named standard ("first profile in 60 minutes", not "fast turnaround").
- **Accountable:** say what happens if it goes wrong (30-day free replacement, NDA and IP terms, ISO 27001).
- **Plain:** short sentences, active voice, name the role, city and channel. Remove friction up front ("No commitment · No deck").
- **House rules that still apply:** no em or en dashes (AB7's site uses them; our documents don't), and run the no-ai-slop skill as the final gate on any client-facing copy.

| Avoid | Write |
|---|---|
| We leverage cutting-edge solutions to streamline your growth. | We line up three pre-vetted profiles within 60 minutes of your brief. |
| Best-in-class lead generation. | Leads pass a three-point qualification call before they reach your sales team. |
| Trusted by leading brands worldwide. | 140+ clients across the US, UK, Australia, Canada, UAE and New Zealand. |

## Co-branding (Champions delivering under AB7)

1. **White label (default):** AB7 brand only. No Champions, LakeB2B, SPAN, Ampliz or MetricFox names, logos, domains or email signatures anywhere, including file metadata (author, company fields).
2. **Named partner (only if AB7 agrees in writing):** AB7 leads; one line on the back page or closing slide, "Delivery partner: Champions Group", our logo in grey, no larger than half the AB7 badge.
3. **Joint lock-up:** not used unless there is a signed teaming agreement.

Commercial reminder: the vendor playbook flags AB7 as "MOU, no rate card". Before any AB7 proposal includes pricing, confirm the Champions-to-AB7 rate is agreed.

## How to apply this skill

1. Confirm the co-branding mode and the client-facing contact for the signature block.
2. Load the format skill needed (docx, pptx, pdf, frontend-design, etc.) and apply the tokens, type scale and layout above.
3. For HTML, define the colours as CSS variables and load Inter from Google Fonts. For Word and PowerPoint, set theme colours to Blue, Green, Navy Ink, Midnight, Teal, Sky and the theme font to Inter.
4. Embed the logo from the vault file. Warn the user if output will be printed or shown large.
5. Check: no dashes, no invented proof, green used for one action only, contrast pairs from the table.
