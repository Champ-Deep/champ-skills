# Originality: beating the AI-logo look

Public feedback on agent-made logos (r/ClaudeAI, r/BestGitHubRepos, Hacker News, October 2026) agrees on four things:

1. **Good ideas in, good logos out.** The process lifts craft; it does not invent a reason for the mark.
2. **Minimal geometry converges.** "A circle plus a rounded shape" kept producing near-duplicates of other people's AI logos.
3. **The model's house style shows**: rounded geometric sans, soft blue-to-violet, chevrons, nodes on a curve.
4. **People are tired of featureless minimalism** and want one detail with personality.

The test runs confirmed it. Marks scored highest when the idea rested on a true fact about the name, and when they evolved the brand's existing equity.

## Rules for the concept longlist

- **Ground every concept in a fact.** Write the fact next to the one-liner. Examples that worked: IP Momentum, where "p is the physics symbol for momentum" became a lowercase ip mark, and "the Doppler effect is sound from a moving source" became a source dot with rings bunched ahead of it. A concept with no fact behind it ("forward motion", "connection") is a mood, not an idea.
- **Spread the longlist across families.** At least one concept from each of these, then pick the best three across families, not within one:

| Slot | What it is |
|---|---|
| Letter | The initial or the name, built so one detail carries the idea |
| Fact | A real phenomenon, object or practice from the business, reduced to a sign |
| Equity evolution | The current logo's recognisable shape or colour, rebuilt with craft and one new idea. **Mandatory** when the brand already has a logo. |
| Abstract system | One primitive (dot, bar, arc) repeated by a rule, so it seeds patterns and icons |
| Negative space | A second read found in the cut-out |
| Wild card | The idea you would not show a committee first |

- **Kill on prior art early.** Before building, run `search_library.py --subject <thing>` and think of the obvious famous marks in the space. A stopwatch C for a recruitment tool died because Algolia and Fastly already own that territory. Note every kill in the report: it shows the shelf test was real.

## AI-logo tells (fix before showing)

- A plain circle, rounded square or blob with one cut and no fact behind it
- An upward chevron or arrow standing for "growth" or "momentum"
- Dots or nodes on a curve standing for "network" or "pipeline"
- A soft blue or violet gradient used to make a weak form feel finished
- An unmodified geometric sans wordmark with the symbol bolted on its left
- A sound-wave bar chart for anything voice-related
- Everything at the same weight with nothing ownable

If a concept shows two of these tells, push it further or drop it.

## Honest ratings

Rate each concept 1 to 10 against a professional identity designer's first concept round, not against other AI output. In the test runs, the agents' self-ratings (6.5 to 7) tracked reality better than the vision model's (9). Report the honest number. A 7 with a clear risk is useful; an inflated 9 is not.
