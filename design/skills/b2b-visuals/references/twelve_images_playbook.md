# The 12 Growth Visuals — Per-Image Spec

Each image is standalone — one use case, one dominant message, the client's actual ICP, the client's name in bold. No client logo unless explicitly requested. "Powered by LakeB2B" appears discreetly on every image. Landscape 16:9. Premium visual system: strong hierarchy, large readable headline, one realistic interface mock-up or diagram, consistent typography, client-relevant color accents — never a generic stock-style layout.

Headline placeholders below use `[Client Name]`, `[Priority ICP]`, etc. — fill from the ICP summary (SKILL.md Step 2), never leave a placeholder unfilled in the rendered image.

---

## Image 1 — Personalized Email Campaign
**Headline:** "How [Client Name] Can Reach [Priority ICP] Through Personalized Email"
**Show:** target ICP, target job titles, target geography, a personalized email subject + body with personalization fields, a call to action, and a compact campaign-workflow diagram (data → send → meeting).

## Image 2 — LinkedIn and Social Outreach
**Headline:** "Engage [Target Decision Makers] Across Social Channels"
**Show:** LinkedIn connection request, LinkedIn follow-up message, a sponsored LinkedIn ad mock-up, optionally a Meta/Facebook ad mock-up **only when the ICP fits** (SMB owners, clinicians, retail, hospitality, education, other consumer-adjacent buyers — skip Meta entirely for enterprise/technical B2B ICPs), target audience filters, and the outreach sequence order.

## Image 3 — AI-Agent Telemarketing
**Headline:** "Turn Verified B2B Contacts into Qualified Conversations"
**Show:** an AI calling interface, a client-specific opening line, 3–6 qualification questions, call disposition categories (interested / not interested / call later / wrong contact / referred / qualified / meeting booked), the meeting-booking flow, human handoff point, and a compliance/opt-out note.

## Image 4 — SEO and AEO Visibility Opportunity
**Headline (client absent):** "Help [Client Name] Be Found Where Buyers Search and Ask AI"
**Headline (client present, not leading):** a variant naming the client's real rank, e.g. "Buyers See 4 Other Names Before [Client Name] — Here's the Search That Proves It"
**Show:** the real buyer search query, a search-results-style mock-up, competitor visibility, the client's real visibility position (from a live, dated search — see SKILL.md Step 3), and recommended content themes (comparison pages, FAQs, expert content, schema/structured data). If a live result genuinely isn't available, label the whole mock-up **"Illustrative search visibility analysis"** rather than presenting it as real. Never fabricate a screenshot of an actual search engine or AI product UI.

## Image 6 — KOL and Buying-Committee Identification
**Headline:** "Map Every Stakeholder Influencing the Purchase"
**Show:** economic buyer, technical buyer, business sponsor, procurement, operations, legal/compliance/infosec where relevant, internal champion, end users, and external KOLs/consultants/analysts — with roles tailored to the client's actual industry, not a generic set.

## Image 7 — Data and Industry Opportunity Analysis

## Visual quality bar (applies to all 12)

- Premium corporate design, strong hierarchy, large readable headline, one dominant message per image
- Realistic interface mock-ups / diagrams / dashboards — not generic stock-photo-style layouts
- Client-specific color accents derived from the client's own website, not a fixed default palette
- Consistent typography and spacing across the full set of 12
- No unreadably small text, no repetitive layouts, no random/unlabeled numbers, no fake testimonials, no fake customer logos

## What NOT to do

- Don't output one combined 12-panel collage when 12 separate images were requested
- Don't carry an ICP, statistic, or visual concept over from a previously analyzed client
- Don't use a subsidiary's ICP for the parent company (or vice versa) without evidence from that specific company's own site
- Don't use any company logo without the person explicitly requesting it
- Don't reach for generic automotive/healthcare/tech/finance stock imagery that doesn't match the actual client's industry
