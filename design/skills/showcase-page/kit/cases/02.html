Challenge and approach

The product fits brands with 50 or more locations. Nobody had checked the list against that.

The first job was to take every account that could never buy off the list before outreach started.

The challenge

691 accounts that no one had screened

The client handed over 691 target accounts for a Southeast Asia pilot. Its product only fits brands with 50 or more locations in six industries, and the list had never been checked against that rule.

50+ locationsRetailQuick service restaurantsAutomotiveHealthcareHospitalityProfessional services
Step 1

Screen every account

We checked each account on location count and industry. 417 passed. The other 274 came off the list, so no calling time went on them.

Step 2

Map the buyers in each eligible account

Inside the 417 eligible accounts we mapped decision makers in Marketing, Customer Experience and Operations, from manager to C-suite.

MarketingCustomer ExperienceOperationsManager to C-suite
Step 3

Release only qualified contacts

We found 1,862 contact profiles and released the 1,211 that qualified, split across the Philippines, Malaysia, Singapore and Indonesia.
