## Style Library (50+ Named Styles)

### Group 1: Minimal / Clean

**1. Swiss Minimal**
- Hallmarks: Helvetica-adjacent typefaces, strict grid, pure black + white, generous whitespace
- When to use: Design tools, developer products, high-end B2B
- Color: Neutral with single accent. No gradients.
- Typography: Neo-grotesque sans. One weight contrast.
- Key DON'T: No decoration, no drop shadows, no rounded corners

**2. Neo-Grotesque**
- Hallmarks: Tight letter-spacing on headlines, asymmetric layout, restrained palette
- When to use: Agency sites, premium SaaS, editorial products
- Color: Off-whites, cool grays, one bold accent
- Typography: Condensed grotesque + body weight contrast

**3. Warm Tech**
- Hallmarks: Cream/tan backgrounds, humanist typefaces, rounded corners, warm shadows
- When to use: Consumer AI, productivity apps, friendly SaaS
- Color: Warm neutrals (amber-tinted), soft accent
- Typography: Humanist sans + slab or transitional serif

**4. Clean Corporate**
- Hallmarks: 12-column grid, nav + sidebar, conservative palette, trust signals
- When to use: Enterprise SaaS, B2B, healthcare, finance
- Color: Blue-tinted neutrals, corporate blue/green
- Typography: Professional sans with tabular numbers

**5. Soft Minimal**
- Hallmarks: Near-white backgrounds, thin weights, very subtle shadows, no heavy borders
- When to use: Portfolio, personal sites, lifestyle apps
- Color: Muted, washed-out palette. One gentle accent.
- Typography: Light weights, generous line-height

### Group 2: Bold / Expressive

**6. Brutalist**
- Hallmarks: Exposed borders, monospace type, stark contrast, raw HTML aesthetic
- When to use: Developer tools, creative projects, anti-brand statements
- Color: Black + white primary, single neon accent optional
- Typography: Monospace or slab serif
- Key DO: Make the brutalism intentional, not accidental

**7. Neo-Brutalist**
- Hallmarks: Thick borders (2-4px), offset drop shadows, flat colors, bold type
- When to use: Startups, Saas tools wanting personality, creative B2B
- Color: Primary palette + black borders. High saturation.
- Typography: Display sans with bold weight contrast

**8. Maximalist**
- Hallmarks: Layered elements, mixed fonts, complex grids, rich textures
- When to use: Fashion, music, entertainment, creative campaigns
- Color: Multiple accents, gradients allowed, chromatic complexity
- Typography: Mixed typefaces intentionally — contrast is the point

**9. Bold Editorial**
- Hallmarks: Full-bleed images, massive type, two-column editorial grid
- When to use: Media, publishing, content marketing
- Color: Black + one ink color, photography-forward
- Typography: Display headline + body serif

**10. Typographic**
- Hallmarks: Type IS the design. Minimal imagery. Scale and weight as visual elements.
- When to use: Law firms, consultancies, premium agencies
- Color: Near-monochrome. Let typography create visual interest.
- Typography: Variable fonts or multiple weights of one family

### Group 3: Dark / Night

**11. Dark Mode Native**
- Hallmarks: Dark gray (not black) surfaces, elevation via lightness, muted accents
- When to use: Developer tools, code editors, night-mode apps
- Color: Dark surface scale (oklch 10–25%), desaturated accents
- Typography: Reduce weight vs light mode equivalent

**12. Cyberpunk**
- Hallmarks: Neon on dark, scan lines, glitch effects, grid overlays
- When to use: Gaming, crypto, sci-fi products
- Color: Near-black + cyan + magenta/pink
- Typography: Condensed, technical, or pixel fonts

**13. Terminal Hacker**
- Hallmarks: Monospace everything, phosphor green/amber on black, command-line aesthetic
- When to use: Security tools, developer CLI, infrastructure products
- Color: Black (#080808) + single phosphor color (green, amber, or cyan)
- Typography: Monospace only

**14. Cinematic Dark**
- Hallmarks: High-contrast photography, minimal UI chrome, full-bleed dark
- When to use: Streaming, film, premium entertainment
- Color: Near-black + single accent, photography-dominant
- Typography: Elegant serif or refined sans

**15. Dark Glass**
- Hallmarks: Translucent panels on dark gradients, subtle blur, depth
- When to use: Dashboard hero states, loading screens — not full interfaces
- Color: Dark gradient base, glass panels with 10-20% opacity
- CAUTION: Overuse creates visual noise. Use sparingly.

### Group 4: Retro / Nostalgic

**16. Retro-Futuristic**
- Hallmarks: 1960s-70s space-age aesthetic, atomic design motifs, optimistic colors
- When to use: Creative campaigns, music, unique brand differentiation
- Color: Warm oranges, electric blues, white. Mid-century palette.
- Typography: Geometric sans or rounded display

**17. Vaporwave**
- Hallmarks: Pink/purple gradients, grid horizons, chrome text, retrofuturist nostalgia
- When to use: Music, nightlife, retro gaming, creative projects
- Color: Gradient from hot pink to purple/teal
- Typography: Italic display + 80s-feel fonts

**18. Analog / Paper**
- Hallmarks: Textured backgrounds, ink-bleed effects, print-inspired layouts
- When to use: Publishing, notes apps, indie products, writing tools
- Color: Cream/parchment + ink black + aged paper tones
- Typography: Old-style serif or monolinear hand-drawn

**19. Retro Pixel**
- Hallmarks: Pixel art, 8-bit color palettes, chunky UI elements
- When to use: Games, nostalgia products, indie developers
- Color: Limited palettes (8-16 colors)
- Typography: Pixel or bitmap fonts

**20. Art Deco**
- Hallmarks: Geometric ornament, gold accents, symmetry, luxury verticality
- When to use: Hotels, jewelry, luxury fashion, premium events
- Color: Black + gold + cream. Deep jewel tones.
- Typography: Tall, condensed display serif

### Group 5: Organic / Natural

**21. Organic**
- Hallmarks: Flowing shapes, earthy palette, imperfect textures, nature motifs
- When to use: Wellness, food, sustainability brands, yoga
- Color: Earth tones (ochre, moss, terra cotta, sage)
- Typography: Humanist serif or handwritten display

**22. Scandinavian**
- Hallmarks: Light woods, muted naturals, functional minimalism, cozy warmth
- When to use: Home goods, furniture, lifestyle, health
- Color: White/cream + pale wood tones + one warm accent
- Typography: Clean sans with humanist character

**23. Tropical / Vibrant**
- Hallmarks: Bold botanical patterns, vivid accent colors, energetic layouts
- When to use: Food & drink, tourism, lifestyle events
- Color: High-saturation tropical — coral, lime, yellow, azure
- Typography: Bold display + clean body

**24. Earthy Craft**
- Hallmarks: Kraft textures, hand-lettered type, rustic details
- When to use: Coffee shops, artisan food, indie brands
- Color: Browns, tans, forest greens, muted reds
- Typography: Hand-drawn display + body serif

### Group 6: Luxury / Premium

**25. Luxury Minimal**
- Hallmarks: Extreme whitespace, thin weights, restrained typography, no decoration
- When to use: Fashion, jewelry, premium consumer goods
- Color: White/cream + black + one precious metal accent
- Typography: Elegant serif display + neutral sans body

**26. High Fashion**
- Hallmarks: Full-bleed photography, editorial layout, asymmetric composition
- When to use: Luxury fashion, fragrance, premium lifestyle
- Color: Black + white as primary. Color in photography only.
- Typography: Refined serif, often capitalized

**27. Auction House**
- Hallmarks: Serif-forward, catalog aesthetic, white backgrounds, formal hierarchy
- When to use: Art galleries, auctions, rare collectibles
- Color: White + black + aged paper tones
- Typography: Old-style serif throughout

**28. Premium Dark**
- Hallmarks: Dark backgrounds, gold/silver accents, refined details
- When to use: Spirits, watches, premium automotive
- Color: Deep background + precious metal accent + photography
- Typography: Condensed display serif + body sans

### Group 7: Functional / Technical

**29. Dashboard Industrial**
- Hallmarks: Dense data, muted palette, technical grid, measurement aesthetics
- When to use: Analytics, monitoring, infrastructure tools
- Color: Dark neutrals, amber/green status colors
- Typography: Tabular numerals, condensed sans

**30. Material Design**
- Hallmarks: Elevation with shadows, color-forward, consistent motion, fab buttons
- When to use: Android apps, Google-ecosystem products
- Color: Material 3 dynamic color system
- Typography: Google fonts, type scale

**31. iOS Human Interface**
- Hallmarks: Translucent navigation, SF Symbols, system fonts, springs
- When to use: iOS native apps, Apple-ecosystem products
- Color: SF Color palette, semantic system colors
- Typography: SF Pro, dynamic type

**32. Flat Design**
- Hallmarks: No shadows, no gradients, bold solid colors, simple icons
- When to use: Illustrations, icons, simple apps, email templates
- Color: Flat color palette, high saturation
- Typography: Geometric sans

### Group 8: Playful / Consumer

**33. Playful**
- Hallmarks: Rounded corners, bubbly type, bright colors, joyful micro-interactions
- When to use: Children's products, games, consumer apps
- Color: Primary + secondary bright palette, no dark
- Typography: Rounded sans or friendly display

**34. Memphis**
- Hallmarks: Geometric patterns, mixed colors, random shapes, 80s-90s energy
- When to use: Creative agencies, trendy consumer brands, events
- Color: Primary colors + black + pattern fills
- Typography: Mixed, expressive

**35. Soft Pastel**
- Hallmarks: Low-contrast pastels, rounded everything, gentle shadows
- When to use: Mental health, women's health, children, wellness
- Color: Pastel pink, lavender, mint, peach — low chroma
- Typography: Rounded sans, gentle weights

**36. Hand-Drawn**
- Hallmarks: Sketch lines, imperfect shapes, handwriting fonts
- When to use: Educational content, personal brands, indie products
- Color: Washed watercolor palette
- Typography: Handwritten display + clean body

### Group 9: Experimental

**37. Glassmorphism**
- Hallmarks: Frosted glass panels, blur effects, light border
- When to use: Hero sections, modals — NOT full interfaces
- Color: Color underneath shows through glass
- CAUTION: Accessibility risk. Test contrast carefully.

**38. Neumorphism**
- Hallmarks: Extruded/inset shapes same color as background, soft dual shadows
- When to use: Specific UI controls — NOT full interfaces
- CAUTION: Accessibility risk. Very low contrast by design.

**39. Duotone**
- Hallmarks: Photography treated with two-color overlay
- When to use: Campaign pages, hero sections, consistent image treatment
- Color: Two complementary or analogous hues for images

**40. Gradient Mesh**
- Hallmarks: Complex mesh gradients as backgrounds, color aurora effects
- When to use: AI/tech marketing, hero sections
- Color: Multi-stop mesh gradients — avoid default AI cyan+purple

**41. Data Ink**
- Hallmarks: Data IS the design. Charts, numbers, and tables as primary visual elements.
- When to use: Analytics products, research tools, dashboards
- Color: Muted base + semantic data colors only

### Group 10: Cultural / Regional

**42. East Asian Minimal**
- Hallmarks: CJK-aware spacing, vertical rhythm option, ink aesthetic
- When to use: Products for Japanese, Chinese, Korean markets
- Color: White/paper + black ink + single seasonal accent
- Typography: Variable-weight CJK-compatible typefaces

**43. South Asian Vibrant**
- Hallmarks: Rich jewel tones, intricate pattern motifs, celebratory palette
- When to use: Cultural events, ethnic fashion, regional food
- Color: Saffron, vermillion, emerald, gold
- Typography: Display with cultural character

**44. Latin Energy**
- Hallmarks: Bold warm colors, expressive type, kinetic layouts
- When to use: Latin music, food, events, lifestyle brands
- Color: Warm saturated palette — terracotta, lime, vivid coral
- Typography: Expressive display + clean body

**45. Nordic Dark**
- Hallmarks: Long-shadow colors (dark for much of the year), cool grays, stark
- When to use: Scandinavian tech products, Nordic brands
- Color: Ice blues, slate, charcoal, occasional warm accent
- Typography: Clean grotesque sans

**46. Middle Eastern Geometric**
- Hallmarks: Geometric pattern tiles, calligraphic type accents, rich colors
- When to use: Regional brands, luxury hospitality, cultural products
- Color: Deep jewel tones, gold, arabesque-inspired
- Typography: Arabic-compatible with strong display presence

### Group 11: Additional Styles

**47. Corporate Sharp**
- Hallmarks: 90-degree corners, strict alignment, formal hierarchy, authority
- When to use: Legal, finance, government, consulting
- Color: Navy, slate, corporate blue, conservative
- Typography: Professional serif or corporate sans

**48. Cold Precision**
- Hallmarks: Tight grid, technical precision, no warmth, pure functionality
- When to use: Medical devices, engineering, scientific instruments
- Color: Cool neutrals, clinical whites, single status accent
- Typography: Technical sans, tabular numerals

**49. Warm Analog**
- Hallmarks: Grain textures, warm off-whites, imperfect details, tactile feel
- When to use: Photography, music, artisan products
- Color: Warm neutrals, photographic tones
- Typography: Slightly imperfect serif or humanist sans

**50. Industrial**
- Hallmarks: Metal textures, dark grids, heavy weights, functional brutality
- When to use: Construction, manufacturing, heavy industry
- Color: Dark steel grays, safety orange/yellow accents
- Typography: Heavy condensed or monospace

**51. Academic**
- Hallmarks: Footnotes, endnotes, wide margins, scholarly hierarchy
- When to use: Research tools, textbooks, academic publishing
- Color: White + cream + black + one conservative accent
- Typography: Old-style serif throughout

**52. Broadsheet**
- Hallmarks: Multi-column newspaper grid, headline hierarchy, bylines, section rules
- When to use: News, journalism, commentary, longform
- Color: Black + newsprint cream + accent (editorial color)
- Typography: Strong display + text-optimized body serif

**53. Cinematic Light**
- Hallmarks: Full-bleed photography (light), minimal UI, film-grain texture
- When to use: Film festivals, photography portfolios, premium lifestyle
- Color: Warm whites, film tones, photography-dominant
- Typography: Elegant display + refined body

**54. Augmented Reality**
- Hallmarks: Wireframe overlays, targeting UI, HUD elements, technical precision
- When to use: AR apps, spatial computing, gaming HUDs
- Color: Translucent overlays on real-world backdrop, accent glow
- Typography: Technical condensed

**55. Kawaii**
- Hallmarks: Pastel + cream, chibi characters, excessive roundness, sparkles
- When to use: Japanese-inspired consumer apps, stationery, cute brands
- Color: Sakura pink, cream, baby blue, lavender
- Typography: Rounded, bubble-like

**56. Cottagecore**
- Hallmarks: Floral patterns, linen textures, warm pastoral palette
- When to use: Lifestyle brands, sustainable products, rural aesthetics
- Color: Dusty rose, sage green, linen cream, terracotta
- Typography: Handwritten or slab serif with character

**57. Solarpunk**
- Hallmarks: Lush green + solar gold, utopian optimism, organic + tech fusion
- When to use: Sustainability brands, climate tech, optimistic futures
- Color: Verdant greens, solar golds, sky blues
- Typography: Friendly humanist with organic character
